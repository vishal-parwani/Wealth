Verdict

The market leader, offered at a discount to its listed peers. SBI Funds Management runs India's largest mutual fund book (₹12.5 lakh cr MF QAAUM, 15.3% share; ~₹29.5 lakh cr total AUM including EPFO/ETF mandates), is debt-free, and earns an ~82% EBITDA margin with 51% ROE. At the top of the band it asks ~36–38× FY26 earnings versus HDFC AMC at ~42×, ICICI Pru AMC at ~49× and Nippon at ~51× — rare for an IPO to price below the peer set. The offer is a pure OFS (SBI + Amundi selling ~₹9,813 cr), so no money enters the company, and AMC economics face a slow regulatory grind on fees. Quality franchise, sensible price: subscribe with a long-term holding intent, not just for the listing pop (GMP ~₹92–100, ~15–17%).

Why it's interesting

  • Scale leadership with a moat: #1 by MF QAAUM (15.3% share) riding SBI's unmatched branch distribution — the structural SIP/financialisation story in its purest listed form.
  • Best-in-class cost engine: lowest opex ratio among the top-10 AMCs (0.08% of QAAUM vs 0.10–0.25% industry), which is why margins and ROE lead the sector.
  • Priced below peers: 36–38× vs a ~42× listed-peer average — the discount plus leadership rarely come together at IPO.
  • Marquee anchor book: GIC, ADIA, BlackRock, Fidelity, Goldman Sachs AM, Norges Bank, Capital Group. Anand Rathi, Swastika and Arihant all rate it Subscribe (long term).
  • Beyond mutual funds: PMS, AIFs, offshore advisory and the giant EPFO/ETF mandates diversify the AUM base (total ₹29.5 lakh cr).

Risks & watch items

  • 100% OFS: not a rupee goes to the company — SBI and Amundi are monetising. Fine for an AMC (no capital needs), but note who's selling at this price.
  • Fee-compression treadmill: SEBI's TER glide path keeps squeezing blended yields as AUM slabs grow; revenue grows slower than AUM. Any fresh TER review is the key regulatory overhang.
  • Market-linked earnings: revenue is a % of AUM — a bear market cuts both AUM and flows simultaneously. EPFO/ETF money is huge but ultra-low-yield.
  • Passive shift & competition: index funds and new-age distributors compress the high-margin active-equity mix over time.
  • Post-listing supply: further stake sales by SBI/Amundi to meet public-shareholding norms could cap the stock.
