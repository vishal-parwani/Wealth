A new active fund-of-funds giving single-ticket exposure to equity, debt, gold and silver by allocating across ICICI Prudential's own active schemes and commodity ETFs, with the allocation actively shifted by asset class based on valuations and macro. The pitch is genuine — hands-off, tax-efficient rebalancing across four asset classes under one of India's strongest fund houses. The catch is that it is (a) brand new with no record, and (b) an extra wrapper over funds you could largely buy directly.

03Strategy & what it holds

Mechanism. The FoF does not buy stocks/bonds directly — it buys units of other ICICI Pru active schemes plus Gold and Silver ETFs, and actively tilts the mix. Framework: 30–80% active equity schemes, 10–60% active debt schemes, 10–30% gold + silver ETFs.

Active equity 30–80%Active debt 10–60%Gold ETF part of 10–30%Silver ETF part of 10–30%

Because holdings are other funds, the "portfolio" is effectively the underlying schemes in §02 plus commodity ETFs — a genuinely diversified, multi-asset book rather than a single-strategy equity bet. Active FoF (vs the AMC's existing Passive Multi-Asset FoF) means the underlying are actively managed schemes, not index funds — higher potential alpha, higher cost.

04Cost, structure & tax
  • Expense stacking. A FoF charges its own expense ratio on top of the underlying schemes' costs. SEBI caps total FoF expenses, but you still pay more than holding a single active fund directly — the convenience of auto asset-allocation is the trade.*
  • Taxation. Multi-asset FoF taxation depends on equity/debt/other mix and holding period and has changed with recent Budget rules — likely taxed less favourably than a ≥65%-equity fund. Confirm the exact tax treatment in the SID before investing.*
  • Rebalancing edge. The key structural benefit: the manager rebalances across asset classes inside the fund without triggering your capital-gains — a real advantage vs holding separate equity/debt/gold funds and rebalancing yourself.
Verify

06Risks
  • No track record. New fund; you're betting on the manager/AMC and the model, not delivered results.
  • Cost drag. FoF expense stacking lowers net returns vs owning the underlying directly.
  • Tax uncertainty. Multi-asset FoF tax treatment is nuanced and may be less favourable than equity funds — confirm.
  • Redundancy. If you already hold ICICI Pru Multi-Asset Fund or a diversified book, this adds little.
  • Allocation risk. Active asset-allocation calls can be wrong; wide bands mean outcomes depend heavily on manager timing.
