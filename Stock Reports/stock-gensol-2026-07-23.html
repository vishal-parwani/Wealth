Once a market-darling solar-EPC and EV-leasing play (tied to sister firm BluSmart), Gensol collapsed in 2025 after SEBI found the promoter-directors, brothers Anmol & Puneet Singh Jaggi, had diverted a large part of ₹978 Cr of IREDA/PFC loans for personal use. The stock is down >90%, the promoters have resigned and are barred from the markets, and the company is in insolvency. The "0.8x P/E / 0.13x P/B" screen is a textbook value trap — the assets and earnings behind those ratios are exactly what the forensic audit is unwinding.

02Business & Qualitative

What it was. Gensol operated two linked businesses: (i) solar EPC — engineering, procurement & construction of solar power projects, plus advisory/O&M; and (ii) EV leasing/manufacturing — financing and leasing electric vehicles, notably to ride-hailing affiliate BluSmart (also promoted by the Jaggis). The EV-leasing arm was debt-funded via green/renewable lenders IREDA and PFC.

What broke it. The related-party web between Gensol, BluSmart and the promoters became the conduit for the alleged fraud: loans raised to buy EVs were, per SEBI, partly routed to related parties and promoter personal expenses (incl. high-end real estate) rather than deployed as represented. The "business" today is an insolvent entity run by an interim resolution professional, with frozen accounts and a leadership vacuum.
