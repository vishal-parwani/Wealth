Verdict

The highest-quality pure play on the bearings thesis — fully priced for it. RBC is the premium name in Morgan Stanley's "bearings as a core robotics play" note: US-domiciled, aerospace/defense-weighted, and the kind of engineered-precision supplier that OpenAI's robotics RFP (precision bearings as 1 of 6 critical components) points toward. FY2026 (ended Mar) was excellent: revenue $1.87 bn (+14%), net income $287.6 m (+23%), EPS $9.14, plus the $275 m VACCO acquisition adding space/naval-defense valves. The problem is the multiple: at ~$595 the stock trades at ~65x trailing GAAP earnings, near the top of its 52-week range ($364–668). The robotics demand ramp MS projects is a 2030s story; the 2026 price already borrows from it. Own it on drawdowns, don't chase it here.

Business

Designs and makes highly engineered precision bearings and components — ball, spherical, thin-section and needle-roller bearings, rod ends — for aerospace & defense and industrial markets. A&D content is the differentiator: sole-source or qualified-supplier positions on long-lived platforms give pricing power most bearing makers lack. The Jul 2025 VACCO Industries acquisition ($275 m, from ESCO Technologies) added valves, manifolds and regulators for space and naval-defense programs; Q4 FY26 guidance implied 13–15% total growth (6.4–8.7% organic ex-VACCO).

Robotics angle (why it's on the list)

  • MS forecasts ~300x growth in robot-bearing demand by 2050; a humanoid needs 70+ bearings vs 8–12 for a drone.
  • OpenAI's US-manufacturing RFP named precision bearings a critical component — RBC is the flagship US precision-bearing maker.
  • Bearings face near-zero substitution/obsolescence risk; RBC's precision/thin-section niches are exactly the robot-joint sweet spot.

Risks

Valuation is the risk. ~65x GAAP earnings for ~7–9% organic growth means multiple compression alone could cost 30–40% if industrial sentiment turns; the $364 low was only a year ago.
  • Robotics revenue today is negligible — the thesis pays off over decades, not quarters.
  • A&D cycle dependence (aircraft build rates, defense budgets); VACCO integration risk.
  • USD position sizing / LRS friction for an Indian portfolio; no dividend to wait with.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: a pullback into the $450–500 zone (~50x or below), or concrete robotics-OEM design wins showing up in bookings.
  • Downgrade to Avoid: organic growth slipping below ~5% while the multiple stays elevated.
