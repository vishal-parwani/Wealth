Why this fund

  • Consistent top-3 performer among the large liquid funds on 1Y/3Y/5Y, with one of India's oldest and most institutionally-used liquid franchises (running since 2003, regular plan NAV near ₹6,900 reflects that long compounding history).
  • AMC diversification: different sponsor group (Nippon Life) from Aditya Birla and Axis — spreading a large cash balance across all three caps single-AMC operational risk.
  • Right-sized AUM (~₹32–37k cr): deep enough for institutional-grade liquidity, small enough to be nimble on short-paper positioning.
  • Conservative book: predominantly A1+ CDs/CPs and T-bills, ≤91-day maturity by regulation; pioneer of the instant-redemption facility (up to ₹50k/90% within minutes via IMPS).

Risks & watch items

  • Manager tenure: lead manager Vikash Agarwal took over in Sep 2024 — shorter tenure than the ABSL/Axis desks, though liquid-fund alpha is more process- than person-driven.
  • Rate cycle: forward returns follow short-term rates; expect ~6.4–6.7% run-rate in an easing environment, not the 3Y 7% print.
  • Slightly behind ABSL/Axis on raw returns — its case rests on diversification plus near-identical performance, not on leading the table.
Use case: second/third parking bucket when cash balances are large enough to warrant splitting across AMCs; also the pick if instant redemption matters most. Hold ≥7 days to avoid the graded exit load. Gains taxed at slab rate (debt taxation).
