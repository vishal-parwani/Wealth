The balance-sheet figures predate Encora, which closed on 1 May 2026, and both now mislead in opposite directions. P/BV divides today's market cap, which includes the roughly 10.8 crd shares issued to Encora's sellers, by March equity, which does not. On a rough post-issue book of about ₹26,000 crd it would be nearer 3.1x unreconciled. Net cash is also stale. Q1 FY27 interest more than doubled, from ₹40 cr to ₹87 cr, which matches the up-to-$550 mn bridge or QIP facility used to repay Encora's term debt. Post-deal net debt has not been filed in a source that could be opened, so treat today's balance sheet as n/a until the September half-year statement.

Verdict

Hold. The business case is a Buy and the governance case has half-healed. At ₹1,847 the stock sits 9% below a 35-analyst consensus of ₹2,017, so there is too little upside to buy ahead of the first full Encora quarter, which reports in late October.

What changed since 24 Sep 2026. The price rose from ₹1,786 to ₹1,847 (+3.4%). The rating stays Hold. The main new fact is that on 29 Sep the board appointed Akhil Gupta, former vice-chairman of Bharti Enterprises and chair of 360 ONE WAM, as independent director and chairperson for five years. The NRC chose him unanimously. He replaces the interim arrangement that was due to run until 31 Jan 2027. That meets half of the September upgrade test, a permanent chair. The company also stated that the Bhatt episode concerned the March–April board-evaluation exercise and "did not involve financial statements, financial reporting or other financial matters". Brokers held their Buys through the episode: Motilal Oswal ₹2,200, Prabhudas Lilladher ₹2,120, Jefferies ₹2,040, Emkay ₹2,000. Axis Direct cut its target to ₹1,980 (26x FY28E) on 29 Sep. No Q2 numbers or Sep-quarter shareholding are out yet.

Why not Buy yet. The September test also asked for two quarters of Encora margins and a separately disclosed organic growth rate. Neither exists: Q1 FY27 carried only two months of Encora. Valuation leaves little room to buy ahead of that evidence. The consensus target is +9.2%. Forward P/E is 31.7x FY27 and 25.9x FY28 consensus EPS, a fair price for 20%-plus earnings growth but not a cheap one. The stock is also within 9% of its ₹2,022 high. Q1 showed that the market punishes a beat that falls short of perfect: the shares fell about 6% on a quarter with revenue up 49% and PAT up roughly 50–110% depending on the definition (see Financials).

Why not Sell. The fundamentals are the strongest in Indian mid-cap IT. FY26 revenue grew 36% and PAT 86%. Free cash flow reached ₹1,197 cr, its highest ever. The twelve-month executable order book is $2.23 bn, up 44%. In September management reaffirmed FY27 guidance: EBITDA margin 20.5–21%, EBIT at least 15.5% and FCF above 100% of PAT. It also said Q2 could bring the most large-deal closures in the company's history. Domestic funds hold the stock across 341 schemes, about ₹26,000 cr. For a holder, the answer is to keep the position and not add yet. Q2, due late October, will show whether Encora is accretive at the margin, and that is the trigger in either direction.

Business

Nature of business

Offshore-led IT services in banking and financial services, insurance, and travel and transportation, sold on multi-year contracts. Encora closed on 1 May 2026 for an enterprise value of $2.35 bn. It adds AI-native product engineering, more than 3,100 engineers and Latin American nearshore delivery for US clients, and takes the group to roughly $2.5 bn of annual revenue. Management classifies 86% of revenue as AI-led services, a label that cannot be verified independently.

Moat

Moderate. Domain depth in insurance and travel creates switching costs and some pricing power. Beyond that, IT services is defended by delivery record and scale, not by proprietary technology. Encora adds engineering depth, but integration has to work for that to count.

Order book

$2.23 bn executable over the next twelve months at 30 Jun 2026, up 44%. That is about ₹19,500 crd at ₹87.5/$, or 1.07x trailing revenue of ₹18,195 cr. Q1 TCV was $691 mn. This is firm contracted work, not pipeline. Management has guided Q2 for record large-deal closures.

Risks

Encora integration is the risk that would break the thesis. The deal added about a third to the share count and roughly doubled interest and amortisation. If Encora margins slip below the 20.5–21% group EBITDA guide, or key engineers leave, EPS growth stalls even while revenue grows, and a stock at 32x forward earnings gets marked down hard.
  • Governance. The chair is fixed, but independent seats remain open and the internal-audit episode is recent. Any further departure would reopen the issue.
  • Acquisition-flattered comparisons. Headline growth flatters until Q1 FY28, and organic growth has not been disclosed on its own.
  • PE seller overhang. Advent and Warburg hold Encora consideration shares, about 24% of the registerd. Block sales as lock-ins expire would weigh on the price.
  • Multiple. 42.6x trailing against 21–23x for LTIMindtree and Mphasis. The premium needs growth to continue.
  • Currency and the US cycle. BFSI and travel depend on US discretionary budgets, and a firmer rupee reverses translation gains.
