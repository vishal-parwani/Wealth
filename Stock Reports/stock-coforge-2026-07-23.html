Coforge is a high-growth mid-tier IT services firm that has compounded revenue at high-teen rates and, post the Cigniti merger and the just-closed Encora acquisition, is re-positioning around AI-led engineering. The stock has corrected ~25% off its 52-week high even as FY26 was a record operating year — the central tension this report examines.

02Business & Qualitative

Model. Global IT services & digital engineering, formerly NIIT Technologies (renamed 2020). HQ in Greater Noida (India) and Princeton, NJ. Led by CEO & Executive Director Sudhir Singh; Chairman Basab Pradhan. Revenue is project + managed-services based, concentrated in a few deep-domain verticals rather than spread thin like the large-caps.

Moat. Specialist depth in BFSI, Travel/Transport and Insurance (serves 10 of the top 15 global airlines), reinforced by a strong, consistent large-deal engine and the lowest LTM attrition in the industry (10.8%). The Encora deal adds an AI-native delivery platform (AIVA) and near-shore LATAM talent — management's pitch is a durable "AI-led engineering" differentiator.

Management & execution. Strong inorganic track record: scaled top Cigniti accounts and lifted that unit's EBITDA from 12% to 21% within five quarters of close. 05Valuation

Coforge trades at ~33–35x trailing earnings and ~2.2x book — historically a premium to mid-cap IT peers, justified by superior growth and a higher-quality deal book. On a forward basis the picture softens: consensus ~23x 1-year-forward EPS against a 3-year average of ~34x, which several brokerages frame as attractive versus its own history after the correction.

The caveat: forward EPS embeds Encora accretion assumptions and a near-term ROE dip (see Risks). EV/EBITDA is elevated once the $550mn acquisition debt is included. So "cheap vs own history" and "still richly priced vs the sector" are both true depending on the lens.

09Risks
  • Encora integration. The deal is >30% of Coforge's revenue and adds 25%+ to headcount — a large, execution-heavy bet; integration missteps would hit the whole thesis.
  • Dilution & debt. ~20–21% equity dilution plus $550mn of new debt; near-term ROE may fall to ~9–10% before integration benefits show.
  • Near-term growth air-pocket. Flat sequential organic growth guided for Q1 FY27 (India-govt exit + soft BFS exit run-rate and a senior-leadership transition in BFS).
  • Gen-AI overhang. Management itself flags 25–30% AI productivity headwinds to existing services over 3–5 years; the sector has been de-rated on this fear.
  • Valuation sensitivity. Even after the correction, any growth/margin disappointment leaves limited cushion.
  • Concentration / macro. BFS + Travel ≈ 48% of revenue; Americas 57%; exposed to US discretionary-spend cycles and currency.
10Verdict
Bull case
  • Best-in-class growth + 430 bps FY26 margin expansion, record deal book ($1.75bn, +16.4%)
  • Lowest attrition in the industry; strong, repeatable large-deal engine
  • Encora repositions Coforge as a scaled AI-led engineering player ahead of peers
  • Forward P/E (~23x) below its own 3-yr average; clear FY30 doubling roadmap
Bear case
  • Encora is a big, dilutive, debt-funded integration bet with real execution risk
  • Near-term ROE compression to ~9–10%; flat organic Q1 FY27
  • Headline growth flattered by Cigniti; organic trajectory is more modest
  • Gen-AI headwind + sector de-rating; still not cheap on absolute multiples
