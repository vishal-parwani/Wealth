The post-Encora balance sheet has now been filed, in the 30 Sep 2026 balance sheet in the Q2 factsheet. It replaces the stale March figures used in the last note. Equity capital is ₹88.6 cr at ₹2 face value, which confirms 44.3 cr shares. Equity attributable to shareholders is ₹22,362 crd, which gives a P/BV of 3.8x, not the 6.7x Screener shows on March book. Borrowings are ₹5,646 cr, mostly a dollar loan that the CFO calls a natural hedge. Against cash and bank balances of ₹1,928 cr, net debt is ₹3,718 cr, about 0.96x trailing EBITDAd. That is normal after an all-stock acquisition, but it means the return on equity will look thin for years.

Verdict

Q2 FY27 results (23 Oct). Coforge filed the results at 16:19 IST, after the market had closed. The stock closed the day at ₹1,900.50 (−0.43%), so the market has not yet reacted. The first session to trade on the numbers is Monday 26 Oct.

  • Revenue: ₹6,171 cr (+11.6% QoQ, +53.6% YoY), or $652.2 mn (+10.1% QoQ, +40.1% YoY). Miss. Zee Business research expected ₹6,380 cr (−3.3%), and press reports cite an analyst estimate of $668 mn (−2.4%).
  • Constant-currency growth: +10.9% QoQ. Organic CC growth was +3.8%, the best in five quarters. Encora supplied the remaining ~7 pointsd. The CFO put Encora's Q2 revenue at about $147–148 mn. That is flat on its pro-rated Q1 level but slightly ahead of analysts' $144–145 mn.
  • Margins: EBITDA ₹1,270 cr at 20.6% (Q1 20.3%, Q2 FY26 18.9%). EBIT was 16.0%, flat on the quarter and +134 bps YoY. In line with the roughly 16.1% expected.
  • PAT attributable: ₹588 cr (+13.4% QoQ, +56.5% YoY). Miss against the ₹673 cr expected (−12.6%). EPS was ₹13.3 (+18.3% YoY, Q1 ₹12.3).
  • One-offs that depressed PAT: ₹55.7 cr of Encora integration costs booked as exceptional items, a ₹97.1 cr hedge loss netted from revenue, and ₹145 cr of acquired-intangible amortisation (Q1 ₹112 cr). The first two cost about ₹2.6 per share after taxd, and management says both shrink from Q3.
  • Deals: order intake $1.04 bn, a record (+50.6% QoQ, +102% YoY). It included eight large deals, among them a $230 mn UK public-sector contract. The next-12-month executable order book is $2.38 bn (+45.4% YoY, +6.7% QoQ). Beat.
  • Guidance raised: FY27 EBIT margin goes to 16% from 15.5%. The EBITDA guide stays at 20.5–21% and FCF/PAT at 100%. Management also guided a 17% EBIT margin for Q4 and at least 17% for FY28. The CFO indicated Q4 EPS of "around ₹19–20"; that is a comment, not formal guidance.
  • People: headcount 47,365 (+1,137 QoQ). LTM attrition was 10.4%, flat, and utilisation 82.4%. FCF was $53.8 mn, 89% of PAT. Total DSO including unbilled rose to 110 days from 102 because novating Encora contracts delayed billing. No interim dividend was announced with Q2; Q1 paid ₹4.

Hold. The operating print was strong: record deals, a first margin-guidance raise since the deal, and Encora margins "ahead of plan". But the headline missed, organic growth is about 8% a year rather than double digits, and at ₹1,900 the stock is only 6% below the ₹2,017 consensus target. Keep the position; don't add until the market has priced the miss and Q3 shows the one-offs rolling off.

What changed since 12 Oct 2026. The price rose from ₹1,846.60 to ₹1,900.50 (+2.9%). The rating stays Hold. Q2 met two of the three conditions this note set for an upgrade. Group EBITDA margin was 20.6% with a full quarter of Encora, above the 20% bar, and TCV was $1.04 bn, above the $700 mn bar. It missed the third: organic growth is now disclosed, but it is not in double digits. The 3.8% QoQ organic CC figure is good, yet backing Encora out of reported revenue ($652.2 mn less ~$147.5 mn, or about $505 mn, against $465.5 mn a year ago) gives roughly +8% organic YoYd. The Q1 PAT conflict from the last note is now resolved: Screener reports profit before minority interest (see Financials). The September shareholding and the September MF disclosure are both out. The board also named PwC statutory auditor from the 2027 AGM; S R Batliboi completes the current term and gave an unmodified limited review on Q2.

Why not Buy yet. The valuation already assumes the good news. The stock trades at 32.7x FY27 and 26.7x FY28 consensus EPSd and sits 6% below its ₹2,022 high. Consensus FY27 EPS of ₹58.2 needs H2 EPS of about ₹33 after H1's ₹25.6d, which is consistent with the CFO's ₹19–20 Q4 indication but leaves no room for error. The bull case is that Q4 annualised EPS of about ₹78 puts the stock at about 24x run-rated. That case depends on three things that are promised but not yet seen: hedge losses falling ($7.1 mn in Q3, $1.2 mn in Q4), exceptional costs going to zero, and Encora growing at the group rate from Q3 after a flat Q2. The market has also punished imperfect beats here before: the shares fell about 6% after Q1. A ~13% PAT miss against estimates may do the same on Monday even though the drivers are non-recurring.

Why not Sell. Nothing in the print breaks the thesis. Margin guidance went up, not down. Deal intake doubled year on year and spans EMEA ($485 mn), the Americas ($455 mn) and the rest of the world ($101 mn). EPS still grew 18% after a roughly one-third larger share count, so the deal is already accretive per share before synergies. Net debt is under 1x EBITDA, and Encora integration costs ($17 mn in total, slightly above the $15–16 mn guided) are almost finished. For a holder, the answer is to keep the position. Monday's reaction and Q3 in late January are the next checkpoints, and a pullback toward ₹1,700 with this order book intact would be a buying level.

Business

Nature of business

Offshore-led IT services sold on multi-year contracts. In Q2 the revenue mix by vertical was banking and financial services 25.3%, travel, transport and hospitality 20.2%, healthcare and hi-tech 17.6% (up from 10.0% a year ago, mostly Encora), insurance 12.8%, overseas government 6.5% and others 17.5%. By geography the Americas were 63.1%, EMEA 24.6% and the rest of the world 12.3%. Encora closed on 1 May 2026 at an enterprise value of $2.35 bn. It adds AI-native product engineering and Latin American nearshore delivery, and its Q2 revenue was about $148 mn. The group now runs at about $2.6 bnd of annualised revenue. Fixed-price work is 40.5% of revenue, and management says that is where AI productivity gains should show in margins from Q4. The top 10 clients are 26.4% of revenue and grew 20% YoY.

Moat

Moderate. Domain depth in insurance, travel and financial services creates switching costs and some pricing power. A 95.5% repeat-business rate reflects that. Beyond it, IT services is defended by delivery record and scale, not proprietary technology. Encora adds engineering depth and nearshore reach. Q2's record EMEA intake, including the $230 mn UK public-sector deal, shows the combined platform winning larger contracts.

Order book

$2.38 bn executable over the next twelve months at 30 Sep 2026, up 45.4% YoY and 6.7% QoQ. That is about ₹22,500 crd at ₹94.6/$, or 1.10x trailing revenue of ₹20,381 crd. Q2 order intake was $1.04 bn: EMEA $485 mn, Americas $455 mn, rest of world $101 mn. This is firm contracted work, not pipeline. Management expects about eight more large deals in Q3.

Risks

Encora still has to grow. Its margins are said to be "ahead of plan", but that is not disclosed separately, and its revenue was flat QoQ in its first full quarter. Management promises growth in line with the group from Q3. If Encora stays flat while ₹145 cr a quarter of acquired-intangible amortisation and about ₹84 cr of net interest keep running, EPS stalls at about ₹13–15. That would leave a stock at 33x FY27E EPS well short of the ₹19–20 Q4 run-rate the bull case needs.
  • Organic growth is high single digit. Headline growth of 40–54% is acquisition-flattered until Q1 FY28. Backed out, organic USD growth is about 8% YoYd. That is good for Indian mid-cap IT but not the double digits the multiple implies.
  • Working capital. Total DSO including unbilled and contract assets rose to 110 days from 102 as Encora contracts are novated. FCF conversion was 89% of PAT, against the 100% guide. If novation drags on, FY27 FCF misses its guide.
  • Leverage and hedges. Net debt is ₹3,718 cr, about 1x EBITDA, held mostly in dollars. Hedge losses of ₹97 cr cut Q2 revenue and another $7.1 mn is guided for Q3. A sharp rupee move either way shifts reported numbers.
  • Governance. The chair is settled and the auditor rotation looks routine. Independent seats remain open, and any further departure would reopen the September questions.
  • PE seller overhang. Advent and Warburg hold about 24% of the registerd from the Encora consideration. Lock-in terms are unconfirmed. Block sales would weigh on the price.
  • Multiple and reaction risk. The stock trades at 38.7x trailing and 32.7x FY27E, against 21–23x for LTIMindtree and Mphasis. After Q1 it fell about 6% on an imperfect beat, and Q2 missed PAT estimates by about 13%.
