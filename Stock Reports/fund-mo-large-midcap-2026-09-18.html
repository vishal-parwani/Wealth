04Cost & suitability
  • Cost. Direct expense 0.64% — reasonable for an active equity fund.
  • Who it's for. Aggressive investors with a 7–10 yr horizon and the temperament to sit through 20–30% drawdowns; best deployed via SIP to average the volatility.
  • Portfolio role. A satellite/aggressive sleeve, not the whole equity allocation — pair with a steadier core.
05Risks
  • Concentration. Few stocks → stock-specific and sector risk is high.
  • Style cyclicality. Momentum/quality-growth can underperform sharply in value/defensive phases (see sister fund's 1Y).
  • Valuation. Midcaps are richly valued after a strong run — forward returns may moderate.
  • Recency bias. The stellar 3Y is off a favourable midcap cycle; don't extrapolate 24% p.a.
