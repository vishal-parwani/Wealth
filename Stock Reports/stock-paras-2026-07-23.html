A genuinely differentiated defence small-cap — India's only listed private player in defence & space optics and EMP protection. FY26 saw four straight quarters of rising revenue and a strong Q4 (PAT +74% YoY). The moat is more real than most peers in this cluster; the problem is the same — an eye-watering multiple (~120x P/E, ~15x book) on a tiny earnings base with low ROE.

02Business & Qualitative

Model. Designs and manufactures defence and space optics & optronic systems (sights, periscopes, space-grade optics for satellites/ISRO), plus a Defence Engineering arm spanning electromagnetic-pulse (EMP) protection, defence electronics, heavy engineering and niche sub-systems. Navi-Mumbai-based; founder-led (Chairman Sharad Shah, MD Munjal Shah).

Moat. The strongest differentiation in this small-cap defence set: indigenous space-grade optics is a high-barrier, low-competition niche (import-substitution for satellite/defence optics), and EMP-protection solutions are similarly specialised. These are sticky, design-in, strategically important capabilities — harder to replicate than commoditised electronics or fabrication.

Growth vectors. Premiumisation of the product mix, optics for space programmes, anti-drone / drone interests (via partnerships/JV), and broad defence-indigenisation tailwinds. India's FY26 defence production hit a record ~₹1.78 lakh Cr (+15.6% YoY), with private-sector share rising.
