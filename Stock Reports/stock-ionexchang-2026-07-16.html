Verdict

The right theme, the wrong quarter — wait for the earnings base to stop falling. Ion Exchange is India's six-decade water-treatment leader (resins, membranes, plants, O&M) with an irreplaceable franchise in an obviously structural theme: industrial water reuse, zero-liquid-discharge mandates, municipal treatment. But the P&L is going the wrong way: FY26 income grew just 7% to ₹2,915 cr while Q4 consolidated net profit collapsed 62% to ₹24 cr — project-mix, execution and provisioning pain in the EPC arm. At 47x trailing earnings that are shrinking, the stock is expensive on current numbers even after its long derating. The offsetting signal: order momentum is real — a $52.8 mn (≈₹440 cr) international filtration contract from Hyundai E&C landed 10 Jul 2026 — and Abakkus holds ₹119 cr. The chemicals/resins segment (high-margin, recurring) is the business you actually want; wait for EPC drag to wash out or a price that stops charging growth multiples for shrinking profits.

Update · 3 Aug 2026: Q1 FY27 results due 5 Aug. The question is whether the Q4 collapse (PAT −62%) was a one-off provisioning/EPC hit or the start of a trend. Rating unchanged (Watch / Earnings Reset Underway) — this print decides whether the reset is ending or deepening.

Business

Founded 1964; India's original water-treatment house. Three legs: engineering/EPC (industrial & municipal treatment plants — lumpy, working-capital heavy, the current problem), chemicals (ion-exchange resins, membranes — exported globally, high-margin, recurring), and consumer (Zero B purifiers). Serves refineries, power, pharma, textiles, municipal bodies; O&M contracts add annuity revenue.

Bull case

  • Water scarcity + tightening discharge norms make treatment spend non-discretionary across Indian industry for decades.
  • Resins/membranes franchise competes with Dow/Purolite globally — genuinely hard to replicate, and worth more than the blended multiple implies.
  • International orders accelerating (Hyundai E&C win, Middle East/Africa pipeline) diversify away from Indian municipal payment cycles.
  • Abakkus position (~₹119 cr) suggests the smart money sees the EPC pain as transient.

Risks

47x for negative growth. A Q4 profit drop of 62% with only 7% revenue growth means estimates are still being cut; historically these EPC resets take 3–4 quarters to bottom.
  • EPC execution/provisioning risk — cost overruns and stuck municipal receivables recur in this sector.
  • Segment opacity: strong chemicals results get netted against weak EPC inside consolidated numbers.
  • Consumer (Zero B) subscale against Aquaguard/Kent/HUL Pureit.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Accumulate: two quarters of PAT stabilisation/recovery, or price near ₹350–370 while chemicals momentum holds; a chemicals-segment value-unlock (separate reporting/demerger) would be a strong trigger.
  • Downgrade to Avoid: another guide-down, receivables deterioration, or order-book conversion slipping through FY27.
