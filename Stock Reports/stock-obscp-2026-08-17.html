Verdict

Hold — unchanged, and the reason is now purely price. The prior note rated this Hold, high risk, at ₹688.60 on 13 Aug 2026. The stock is ₹860 — up 25% in four days of trading. The business has done nothing but improve; the entry point has got materially worse.

The operating record is genuinely strong. Q1 FY27 sales ₹77.31 cr, up 84.7%, with PAT ₹9.47 cr, up 88.3%. That is the seventh consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth — ₹34.83 cr in Dec 2024 to ₹77.31 cr now, a 2.2x in six quarters, with margin holding near 18% throughout. FY26 revenue was ₹220 cr against ₹142 cr, up 55%. This is not a story stock; the numbers are compounding.

What holds the rating at Hold. 70.7x trailing for a ₹2,223 cr auto-component supplier prices in the growth continuing at this rate, and auto components is a cyclical industry with customer concentration. Borrowings rose from ₹27 cr to ₹69 cr in FY26 to fund capacity — sensible, but it removes the net-cash cushion. And the ownership picture below is a genuine caution.

Two things to weigh together. Promoters sold 3.69 points in the March 2026 quarter, and FIIs cut from 1.55% to 0.64% in June — while the stock was rising and results were accelerating. Promoter sell-downs into strength are common and often benign (liquidity, diversification, meeting float norms). But a promoter reduction and a foreign exit in consecutive quarters, on a name with no mutual fund ownership at all, is a pattern worth naming rather than passing over.

Only 2,770 shareholders on the register. That is a very small holder base for a ₹2,223 cr market cap and it makes the price highly sensitive to modest flows in either direction.

Business

Nature. Precision metal components — machining, turning, casting, fabrication, stamping and forging — supplied to automotive, defence and marine customers. Six manufacturing plants across Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Haryana. The defence and marine exposure is what differentiates it from a pure auto ancillary and is likely why the multiple is where it is.

Moat. Modest. Precision machining competes on tolerance, quality consistency and delivery; qualification with an OEM takes time and creates stickiness once won. But there are many capable suppliers and the customer holds the pricing power. The defence work carries higher barriers than the auto work.

Peers. Sansera Engineering, Bharat Forge, Endurance Technologies and a long tail of unlisted machining shops. OBSC is far smaller than the listed comparables, which is both the growth opportunity and the fragility.

Order book. Reported above ₹1,200 cr. Against TTM revenue of ₹255 cr this would be exceptional visibility — but no execution window is given and the figure comes from secondary reporting, not a filing. Treat it as indicative.

Risks

  • No institutional floor. Zero mutual fund ownership, 2,770 shareholders, 27.95% float. Price discovery is thin in both directions.
  • Valuation. 70.7x trailing for a cyclical component supplier. The growth justifies a premium; this is a large one.
  • Promoter and FII selling. 3.69 points of promoter reduction and FII holding more than halved, both while results accelerated.
  • Customer concentration. Not disclosed, but a supplier this size typically depends on a handful of OEM relationships. Losing one would be material.
  • Standalone only. No consolidated statements are published, so any subsidiary or related-party activity is not visible in the numbers above.
  • Rising leverage. Borrowings up 2.6x in FY26 to fund capacity. Justified while growth persists; painful if the auto cycle turns mid-expansion.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy — a meaningful price correction bringing trailing P/E under ~45x with growth intact, or the first mutual fund positions appearing alongside continued promoter stability.
  • Downgrade to Sell — further promoter selling, growth decelerating below 30% YoY, or margin slipping under 15%.
  • Stays a Hold — while the operating performance is excellent and the price has already captured it.
