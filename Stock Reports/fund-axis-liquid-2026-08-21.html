Why this fund

  • Lowest cost in the large-fund peer set. In a category where gross portfolios look nearly identical, TER is the most persistent driver of long-run relative return — a ~10 bps annual edge compounds reliably.
  • Best recent momentum: leads the peer set on 3M and 6M annualised returns, suggesting the cost edge is currently translating into headline outperformance.
  • Strong risk-adjusted profile: Sharpe ratio ~4.2, among the top of the peer group.
  • Clean, conservative book: A1+ CDs/CPs, T-bills and short sovereign paper; 91-day maximum maturity per SEBI rules; ~₹56,000 cr AUM provides deep liquidity.

Risks & watch items

  • Rate cycle: as with all liquid funds, forward returns follow short-term rates downward in an easing cycle; expect ~6.5% run-rate, not the 7% 3Y print.
  • AMC transition history: Axis MF went through management churn in 2022–23 (equity-desk issues, unrelated to the debt desk); the current fixed-income team (Devang Shah) has been stable.
  • TER quoted by aggregators varies 0.10–0.12% by date; verify the current figure in the latest factsheet.
Use case: the cost-optimised choice for larger, longer-parked balances where the TER edge compounds. Redemptions settle T+1; instant-redemption facility available. Hold ≥7 days to avoid the graded exit load. Gains taxed at slab rate (debt taxation).
