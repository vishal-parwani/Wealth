Verdict

The cleanest earnings momentum in the Abakkus six. Carysil — one of only four companies globally with proprietary quartz-composite sink technology — grew FY26 PAT 54% to ₹98.2 cr on 13.3% revenue growth to ₹924 cr, as margin recovery and operating leverage kicked in across its six plants. It is a genuine export franchise (OEM for global brands plus own-brand Carysil in the UK/US/Europe) riding the "China+1" kitchen-products shift, with steady expansion into stainless sinks, appliances and bath products. At ₹1,210 (~34x trailing) it isn't cheap, but unlike most of this list the multiple is backed by 50%+ earnings growth, analyst targets cluster at ₹1,246–1,424, and the stock is consolidating just under its ₹1,248 high after a 65% recovery off the ₹732 low. Accumulate in tranches; add aggressively on any consumer-durables sell-off.

Update · 23 Jul 2026: Price roughly flat (~₹1,170–1,210). Q1 FY27 results due (Jul/Aug) — the key read on whether FY26's 54% PAT growth sustains. Rating unchanged (Accumulate).

Business

Formerly Acrysil. Core: quartz composite sinks (German Schock technology heritage — one of four such manufacturers worldwide), exported OEM to major global kitchen brands, plus own-brand retail in UK (Carysil UK), US and Middle East. Extensions: stainless-steel sinks, built-in kitchen appliances (hobs, ovens, chimneys), and bath quartz products — same channel, higher wallet share. Export-heavy mix with growing India premium-kitchen distribution.

Bull case

  • PAT +54% with margins still normalising — FY27 consensus growth doesn't require heroics.
  • Structural moat: quartz-sink manufacturing know-how is a four-player global club; switching costs for OEM customers are high.
  • China+1 and US/UK housing-renovation demand both push volume toward Indian capacity.
  • Abakkus #3 position (~₹177 cr) in a ₹3,400 cr company is a significant ownership signal.

Risks

Export cyclicality. UK/US/Europe kitchen demand tracks housing and rate cycles; FY24 showed how fast export orders can pause. A US tariff shock or freight spike hits margins directly.
  • Currency: meaningful GBP/USD/EUR revenue vs INR costs — hedging imperfections cut both ways.
  • Customer concentration among large OEM kitchen brands (IKEA-type relationships carry re-negotiation risk).
  • At 34x, a growth stumble to ~15% PAT growth would mean dead money for a year-plus.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: a dip toward ₹1,000–1,050 (~28x), or appliance/bath segments reaching scale profitability, diversifying the sink dependence.
  • Downgrade to Watch: export order softness or two quarters of sub-20% PAT growth.
