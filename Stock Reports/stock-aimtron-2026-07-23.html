A Vadodara-based electronics design & manufacturing (ESDM/EMS) micro-cap riding India's electronics-manufacturing wave. FY26 revenue +89% with a ₹521 Cr order book (~1.7x revenue) and aggressive US/Europe expansion. Genuine growth — but it's an NSE SME listing (lower liquidity, lighter disclosure) at a rich multiple, which dominates the risk profile.

02Business & Qualitative

Model. Full-stack ESDM — PCB design & assembly through to complete box-build systems, serving network-security, green-energy, industrial-IoT and AI-linked customers across India, the US, UK, Spain, Mexico and Hong Kong. Two plants (Vadodara, Bengaluru) with SMT/THT/box-build lines. Listed on NSE SME in Jun 2024 (IPO ₹87 Cr, 50% listing pop).

Moat. Limited and early — EMS is a competitive, capital-intensive, margin-thin business where scale and customer stickiness build slowly. Aimtron's edge is a shift toward higher-value ODM / box-build mix and a US manufacturing footprint (rare for a company this size), which it's betting differentiates it on China+1 / "friend-shoring" demand.

Management & expansion. Promoter-led (Chairman Mukesh Jeram Vasani; ~71% holding, no pledge). FY26 added US entities — acquired International Control Services Inc. (Decatur), set up Aimtron LLC (Texas), opened a Germany sales office; US subsidiary order book $7.2mn with a $49.3mn RFQ pipeline.

05Valuation

Trades at ~50–60x earnings and ~10x book — a full multiple for an EMS micro-cap, justified only by the growth rate and order pipeline. EMS peers (Kaynes, Syrma, Avalon) also carry premium multiples, but those are larger, more liquid main-board names. The SME tag means the multiple can both inflate (thin float) and gap down hard (low liquidity).

06Ownership & Dividends

Promoter holding ~70.9%, confirmed unencumbered (no pledge). No dividend — earnings reinvested into capacity and the US build-out. High promoter holding aligns interest but leaves a thin public float (a liquidity risk in both directions).

09Risks
  • SME listing. Lower liquidity, wider spreads, lighter disclosure, sharper drawdowns — the single biggest structural risk.
  • Margin compression from US/Europe ramp costs (EBITDA margin −759 bps in H2).
  • Working capital. ~164 debtor days; growth is cash-hungry.
  • Valuation. ~50–60x P/E on a micro-cap leaves little cushion.
  • Scale & concentration. Small revenue base; customer/segment concentration; competitive EMS economics.
10Verdict
