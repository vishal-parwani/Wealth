Verdict

Hold. If you don't own it, don't buy in the two days before the 31 Oct half-year print, and don't buy at 70x afterwards either unless the half confirms the growth. The business case has improved again since September. Orders filed in October add about ₹103 cr. They include a ₹77.5 cr Tenneco addendum, which takes that nomination to ₹237 cr, and the company's first defence export order: ₹8.9 cr of fuze parts for IMI Systems (Elbit), to ship within six months. AS9100D aerospace certification has also been received. Management guides FY27 revenue growth of 40–45%. Even at that pace the stock sits at about 57–59x FY27 earningsd on the enlarged share count. That leaves no room for a miss on a cyclical, cash-hungry supplier with no fund holder and a ₹4 cr-a-day float.

Why not Buy. (1) Valuation. The trailing P/E is 70.7x on the 2.73 cr shares now in issued. The old Buy test of 45x trailing EPS is about ₹519d, 36% below spot. (2) Growth is paid for with equity. FY26 operating cash flow was −₹2 cr and free cash flow −₹79 cr. Working-capital days rose to 110. The company has issued shares twice in eight months: ₹43.3 cr at ₹311 in Feb 2026 and ₹86.5 cr at ₹601 in Sep 2026. Together that is about 10.5% dilutiond. (3) Much of the order book is soft. The "₹1,200 cr+" figure is mostly multi-year nominations and LOIs, not firm POs, and the new Tenneco tranche does not start until Jan 2027. (4) Overhang. 14.1 lakh shares from the ₹311 issue were still locked at 30 Sep and carry a 2.6x gain. Their release date is not in the filings.

The likely causes are profit-taking after a 1-year rise of about 160% (₹309 → ₹815) and positioning ahead of the H1 print. That is my reading, not a sourced cause. Rating. Unchanged at Hold. No old trigger has fired, as tested below. Corrections to the old note. The March 2026 promoter step-down (73.50% → 69.94%) was not promoter selling. It was dilution from the 13.93 lakh-share preferential issue of 6 Feb 2026 at ₹311. Promoter share count was 1,80,76,000 in June and 1,80,74,400 in September, so it is essentially flat. Business

Nature of business

The company makes precision metal components through machining, turning, casting, fabrication and, since Q1 FY27, stamping. Customers are auto Tier-1s, including Tenneco's suspension business for export to Mexico, plus marine, defence and telecom-infrastructure makers. The six plants are in Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Haryana. Sanand (Gujarat) is under construction, and Supa (Maharashtra) breaks ground about Nov 2026. Stated direction: from component to assembly, and from auto to aerospace and defence. It has AS9100D, is registered with 3 of the 7 DPSUs, and has received prototype orders for MK-84 bomb fins and 155 mm shell lifting plugs.

Peers

Sansera Engineering, Craftsman Automation, Bharat Forge and the long tail of unlisted machining shops. On the defence side: smaller listed precision-component makers. OBSC is a fraction of the size of its listed auto peers and trades at a higher trailing multiple than most of them.

Moat

Modest. OEM and Tier-1 qualification (PPAP, AS9100D, DPSU registration) takes years and makes the business sticky once won. Tenneco has expanded the ₹160 cr nomination it gave in June 2025. But machining capacity is contestable, and the customer holds the pricing power.

Order book

The company states ₹1,200 cr+ (Q1 FY27 release and AGM), about 4.7x TTM revenued. Disclosed wins since August are ₹31.4 cr (PO, to 2032), ₹8.9 cr (PO, 6 months), ₹77.5 cr (nomination, from Jan 2027) and ₹17 cr (LOI, 5 years). Most of the stated book is multi-year nominations, which are volume-dependent programme awards. They are not firm commitments, and no execution schedule is given for the ₹1,200 cr. Treat it as visibility, not backlog. unreconciled

  • Cash burn and equity reliance. FY26 FCF was −₹79 cr and CFO negative. Two preferential issues in eight months (₹130 cr) and higher debt fund the capex. Expect more raises if Supa is built in phases as planned.
  • Overhang. 14.1 lakh shares from the ₹311 issue, at a 2.6x gain, have an unlock date not found in the filings. The promoter lock-in expiry is also undisclosed.

  • Customer concentration and order-book quality. Tenneco is clearly a large customer, but no customer split is disclosed. Nominations are not firm POs.
  • Reporting. Standalone only. Q3 FY26 was filed late (26 Mar), NSE sought Reg 33 clarifications twice, and the company has had two CS changes in 18 months.
