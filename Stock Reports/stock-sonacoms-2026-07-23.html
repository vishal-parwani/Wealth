Verdict

The highest-quality EV-driveline compounder in India — priced like everyone already knows it. Sona Comstar is a global leader in differential gears (6–7% of the world PV differential market) that has successfully pivoted into EV traction motors, driveline and — via acquisitions — mmWave radar/software and railway equipment. FY26 was strong: revenue +26% to ₹4,475 cr, EBITDA margin a fat 24.7%, with a ₹23,700 cr net order book (~5.3x annual revenue, ~70% EV-linked across 67 programmes and 35 customers). The problem is entirely valuation and momentum: after a +63% six-month run to a 52-week high (~₹727), it trades at ~70x earnings — and Bernstein's own raised target of ₹660 sits below the current price. Adjusted PAT grew only 11% while revenue grew 26%, so margins/return ratios are the watch item, not demand. This is a business you want to own and a price you don't want to chase. Watch for a correction toward the high-₹500s / low-₹600s, where the growth is no longer fully pre-paid.

Business

Gurugram-based global auto-tech supplier with plants in India, US, Mexico, China and Serbia. Core: differential gears & assemblies (world top-3) and starter motors, increasingly EV traction motors and driveline (BEV now 39% of revenue). Diversification adds optionality: NOVELIC (Serbia mmWave radar/sensing + mobility software) and the acquired Escorts Kubota railway equipment division (~₹913 cr FY25 revenue, ₹188 cr PBT) — pushing Sona beyond passenger-vehicle cyclicality into rail and sensing. Powertrain-agnostic by design: it sells into ICE, hybrid and BEV platforms alike.

Bull case

  • Order book of ₹23,700 cr (~5.3 years of revenue), 70% EV — visibility few auto-component names anywhere can match.
  • Genuine technology moat and global OEM relationships (33% of auto revenue already EV); wins scale as each program ramps to peak volume.
  • 24.7% EBITDA margin is best-in-class for auto components; diversification into radar/software and railway lowers auto-cycle dependence.
  • Structural EV adoption is a multi-decade tailwind independent of any single geography or OEM.

Risks

The sell-side target is below the price. Bernstein's raised ₹660 target sits under the ~₹727 CMP, and ~70x earnings with only 11% PAT growth means multiple compression is the base-case risk if any program slips or margins stay squeezed.
  • Margin/return-ratio squeeze: PAT grew far slower than revenue in FY26 — new-program ramp costs and mix are pressuring returns; the market is not pricing any disappointment.
  • Customer/geo concentration & US exposure: large US footprint means tariff/policy and OEM-EV-slowdown risk (past BYD program cancellation showed order-book fragility); a US EV-demand air-pocket would hurt.
  • China EV competition compressing global supplier pricing.
  • Acquisition integration (railway, NOVELIC) diversifies but adds execution complexity outside the core.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Accumulate/Buy: a correction toward ₹560–620 (~50–55x) with the order book intact, or return ratios/margins visibly re-expanding as programs mature.
  • Downgrade to Watch-only/Avoid: order-book cancellations, US EV-demand slump, or another year of PAT growth lagging revenue by half.
