Stock Research · Aerospace & Defence · 16 Jul 2026

Dynamatic Technologies Limited

NSE: DYNAMATECH · ~₹6,711 cr mkt cap · Abakkus's largest disclosed holding (~₹206 cr)
Hold

Verdict

India's best aerospace-structures story, trading on the story rather than the earnings. Dynamatic is a Tier-1 supplier of flight-critical airframe structures to Airbus, Boeing and Dassault, with an exclusive AMCA (India's 5th-gen fighter) partnership and a Dassault rear-fuselage agreement — strategic assets almost impossible to replicate. Aerospace revenue grew 27.6% in FY26 and drove total revenue up 15.5% to ₹1,621 cr. But consolidated PAT fell ~25% to ₹32.4 cr (metallurgy/hydraulics drag plus margin pressure), which puts the ₹6,711 cr market cap at a triple-digit multiple of reported earnings (quoted P/E figures of ~83x rely on adjusted/standalone measures — either way it's expensive). Abakkus holding this as its #1 position (~₹206 cr) says the smart money is underwriting the 2030s aerospace ramp, not the FY26 P&L. Fine — but at ₹10,050 with falling PAT, fresh money has no margin of safety. Watch for either a meaningful correction or the earnings inflection.

Update · 3 Aug 2026: Q1 FY27 not yet reported (trading window shut since 1 Jul). Separately, an aggregator has moved the stock to a Sell stance. The defence optionality (Aerodata/Dynauton UAV tie-up, AMCA) is intact, but FY26 PAT fell 25% and the multiple leaves no room for a weak print. Rating unchanged (Watch / Great Story, Rich Price).

Business

Three segments: aerospace & defence (flight-critical assemblies — Airbus A220/A320 flap-track beams, Boeing structures, Dassault Falcon/Rafale-ecosystem work, exclusive AMCA airframe partnership), hydraulics (gear pumps for tractors/industrial — a global #1 niche), and metallurgy (the FY26 problem child). Aerospace is the valuation driver: multi-decade sole-source contracts with rising Indian defence indigenisation content.

Bull case

  • Airbus/Boeing build-rate recovery plus Dassault fuselage work gives compounding, sticky export revenue; AMCA adds a domestic defence option worth potentially decades of orders.
  • Abakkus's largest position — a deep-research house sizing max conviction here (KB-captured tweet, 16 Jul 2026).
  • Operating leverage: aerospace at 27.6% growth carries structurally higher margins as programs mature; metallurgy drag is cyclical, not structural.

Risks

Valuation assumes flawless execution. Reported PAT of ₹32 cr against a ₹6,711 cr market cap means the stock discounts years of future aerospace scaling; any program delay (AMCA timelines are government-dependent) or build-rate cut compresses it hard. Q4 PAT already fell 22–25% and the stock dropped 7% on results.
  • Segment drag: metallurgy losses/weakness can keep masking aerospace strength for several more quarters.
  • High absolute share price (~₹10k) with modest liquidity; concentrated institutional exits are punishing.
  • Customer concentration: Airbus ecosystem dominates; aerospace supply chains punish quality lapses permanently.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: consolidated PAT inflecting decisively upward (₹25 cr+/quarter run-rate), metallurgy stabilised, or a 25–30% price correction with the aerospace book intact.
  • Downgrade to Sell: aerospace growth slowing below ~15% while the multiple stays in triple digits.
