Business

Two engines. Wires & Cables (W&C) — the profit core — spans house wire, power cable and speciality cable, sold through a large retail/electrician channel plus institutional and export routes. FMEG (fans, lighting, switches, appliances) is the consumer-brand extension that has historically lost money and is only now approaching break-even, with premium products at ~20% of FMEG revenue. The declared strategy is explicitly Polycab's playbook: shift mix toward higher-margin cables (targeting ~31% of sales by FY27) while using the brand and distribution to make FMEG self-funding. Reported gross margin ~18.4% and EBITDA margin ~8.1% on the improving mix.

Bull case

  • Both engines firing at once — a rare moment. W&C growth is accelerating (Q4 +36.3%, Q1 revenue +54%) while FMEG stops being a drag rather than staying one.
  • Structural demand: housing, real-estate completions, electrification and data-centre/industrial capex all feed cable volumes; this is the same grid/build-out wave behind Transrail and Laser Power in this library.
  • Credible, specific guidance: 18% FY27 volume growth, 10.5% W&C EBITDA margin by FY28, FMEG +20% p.a. Management has so far delivered against it.
  • Operating leverage is visible — PAT +129% on revenue +54% means margin, not just volume, is doing work.
  • Scale milestone ($1 bn revenue) improves procurement, brand spend and institutional access.

Risks

The price is the risk. +112% in three months into a 52-week high, at ~44x, with the average analyst target 19% below spot. Even a strong FY27 can produce a flat or negative return from here if the multiple normalises. This is the same pattern flagged on Sona Comstar — bullish labels, targets underwater — but more extreme.
  • FMEG is not won yet. Break-even is guided, not banked. Havells and Crompton have decades of supply-chain and brand depth; defending share may demand price cuts or heavy ad spend, either of which eats the margin story.
  • Commodity pass-through: copper and aluminium swings hit cable margins directly, and a favourable input period flatters current numbers.
  • Legal overhang: Q4 FY26 commentary referenced margin hurdles alongside legal matters — worth verifying scale and status in the annual report before sizing a position.
  • Export/geopolitical exposure on the international W&C book.
  • Comparison risk: being valued off Polycab's playbook only works while growth outpaces Polycab; any convergence removes the re-rating rationale.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Accumulate: a pullback toward ₹2,050–2,200 (i.e. into the consensus zone) with growth intact — or analysts revising targets decisively above ₹2,600, which would mean the fundamentals have caught up to the price rather than the reverse.
  • Upgrade sooner if FMEG posts two clean profitable quarters and W&C EBITDA margin tracks visibly toward the 10.5% FY28 goal — that would justify a structurally higher multiple.
  • Downgrade to Avoid: volume growth slipping below the 18% guide, FMEG break-even being pushed out again, or a commodity reversal compressing W&C margin.
