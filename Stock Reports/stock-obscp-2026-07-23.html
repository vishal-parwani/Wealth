A fast-growing Pune-based precision-machining and investment-casting supplier — mostly automotive OEM components today, pushing into defence, marine and telecom-infra. Revenue has nearly doubled in two years (₹115 → ₹220 Cr) with PAT more than doubling, and the market has rewarded it richly: ~61x earnings, ~9x book, up ~6x from its Oct-2024 SME IPO. The tension: a genuinely capable, capacity-adding components maker, but priced like a structural compounder while still small, auto-cyclical and SME-illiquid.

02Business & Qualitative

Model. OBSC (the name traces to Omega Bright Steel & Components) makes high-precision metal components and sub-assemblies to customer design — machining plus investment casting — primarily for automotive OEMs, and is actively diversifying into defence, marine and telecom-infrastructure. Footprint: Pune Unit I (machining, ~11.5 lakh units/yr, 2020), Unit II (investment casting, ~600 MT/yr, 2022), a Chennai machining unit (2023), and a new Chakan Unit IV (2025) — i.e. a real, multi-site capacity build, not a single shed.

Moat. Process- and relationship-based rather than structural: OEM qualification, tight tolerances, investment-casting capability (a useful differentiator vs pure machining shops), and the switching friction of being designed-in. Against that, auto components is competitive and cyclical, customer concentration is a live risk, and the defence/marine diversification is still early-stage optionality rather than proven revenue.

05Valuation

At ₹637 the stock trades on ~61x trailing earnings and ~9x book, 0% yield — a full growth multiple for an auto-heavy components maker. From an Oct-2024 IPO around ₹100 it has compounded ~6.4x in ~20 months, so most of the return is re-rating layered on real growth. For context, larger, established auto-ancillary compounders often trade at 30–50x; a sub-₹250 Cr-revenue SME on 61x is pricing in years of sustained >35% growth and a successful defence/marine diversification. The margin of safety is slim: any auto-cycle softness, margin slippage or capex disappointment can compress the multiple hard, amplified by SME liquidity.

09Risks
  • Valuation. ~61x earnings / ~9x book leaves no margin of safety after a 6x run.
  • Auto cyclicality & concentration. Core revenue tied to auto OEM demand; likely customer concentration; diversification unproven.
  • Capital intensity. Multi-site casting + machining build needs continuous capex & working capital — execution and funding risk.
