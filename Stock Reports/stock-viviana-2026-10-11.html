Verdict

Hold. The growth visibility is real and the multiple is low, but earnings have not become cash in any year on record, and the company is about to issue equity and debt to fund itself. The low P/E reflects that.

Where this name came from. It came from a tweet saved in the knowledge base. On 21 Sep 2026 @xvi_harley wrote that the P/E had fallen from 101 to 13.5 and that the company had 30–50% revenue-growth visibility. Both halves check out, and the growth half understates the guidance. At the bonus-adjusted December 2024 high of ₹688, FY24 EPS of ₹6.82 gives about 101xd. On 21 Sep the stock closed at ₹732.60 against TTM EPS of ₹54.82, which is 13.4xd. Management has guided FY27 revenue to about ₹900 cr, which would be +70% on FY26's ₹531 cr. After the August L1 wins, the company put unexecuted work above ₹1,700 cr. Since the tweet the stock has risen 11.6%, to ₹818.

The evidence is strong on volume. Revenue has gone from ₹66 cr in FY24 to ₹531 cr in FY26. Q1 FY27 revenue rose 129% on a consolidated basis, to ₹72.4 cr. On 10 Oct the company converted its ₹466.7 cr MGVCL Robust-III L1 into a signed contract, to be executed over 16–18 months. ROCE is 49%.

The counter-argument is on cash. Operating cash flow was −₹11, −₹3, −₹14 and −₹4 cr in FY23–FY26, while reported profit summed to ₹84 cr. Receivables reached ₹389 cr at March 2026 against ₹531 cr of revenue, which is 267 debtor days. Borrowings doubled to ₹100 cr. The company has arranged NCDs (the AOA was amended for a debenture-trustee nominee). On 10 Oct the board deferred a preferential warrant issue to a 15 Oct meeting, so dilution is coming at an unknown price. The company sold its real-estate subsidiary to identified purchasers, and on the earnings call management said "the promoters are same". It then sought material related-party limits with that same entity. A Buy rating would need cash collection and a clean promoter interface. Neither is visible yet.

Business

Nature of business

Turnkey EPC for power transmission and distribution up to 400 kV: transmission lines, EHV substations, conversion of overhead lines to underground cable and covered conductors, and live-line work. Customers are mainly the Gujarat utilities (PGVCL, MGVCL, DGVCL, GETCO) and renewable developers. Battery-storage EPC is about ₹500 cr of the book, including a GUVNL BESS at Asoj. A transformer plant at Por started in September 2026. The Gujarat discom Robust-III scheme is the single biggest source of the current book.

Moat

None of substance. Distribution EPC is won by tender, mostly on lowest price. What Viviana has is qualification: its execution history, its 400 kV credentials and its standing with Gujarat utilities. That gets it onto bid lists, not into a protected position. Margins of 14–16% are healthy for EPC and are likely to come under pressure as the bids get larger.

Order book

₹1,312.5 cr confirmed at the 6 Aug 2026 call, about 2.3x TTM revenued. About ₹500 cr of that is BESS, and the figure includes GST. After the August L1 wins (₹113 cr, ₹77.8 cr, ₹275 cr), management said unexecuted work exceeded ₹1,700 cr, including L1 positions. The ₹466.7 cr MGVCL signing on 10 Oct converts part of that L1 pool into firm orders rather than adding to it. A further ₹1,400 cr of bids are under evaluation, and those are not orders.

Risks

Working capital. Receivables of ₹389 cr are 47% of the market cap. Four straight years of negative operating cash flow have been funded with borrowings (₹100 cr), NCDs and soon warrants. If a Gujarat discom slows payment, profit stays on paper and the funding gap widens.
  • Dilution. A warrant issue is due for decision on 15 Oct. If it is priced near the current price it is fine. If it is priced at a discount and taken by promoters, it moves value away from minority holders.
  • Related parties. The real-estate subsidiary went to promoter-linked buyers and new related-party limits were approved with it. Every future transaction with Viviana Life Spaces deserves a read.
  • Back-ended execution. The ₹900 cr guidance needs about ₹276 crd a quarter from here, against a ₹72 cr Q1.
  • Concentration. Most of the order book comes from Gujarat state utilities under one scheme (Robust-III).
  • Surveillance and liquidity. The stock trades in BE (trade-for-trade) series, with about ₹2.35 cr a day of turnover.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: H1 FY27 showing positive operating cash flow and receivable days under 200, plus a warrant issue priced at or above market without a skew to promoters.
  • Downgrade to Sell: warrants issued to promoters at a material discount, receivables rising faster than revenue again in H1, or further related-party transactions with the divested subsidiaries.
