Verdict

Buy, maintained. Q2 cleared every bar the 13 Oct note set, and the capital raise answers the question that capped the stock: how a standalone NBFC with 12.5% Tier I funds 25% growth. Blackstone pays ₹602, about what the market paid before the news. Book value per share rises 13% and leverage falls from 5.5x to 3.9x. EPS is diluted by 12–20% until the money is lent out. At ₹627 the stock is 1.53x pro-forma book and about 8.7x diluted FY28 earnings, which is cheap for a lender guiding to an 18–20% ROE.

What changed since 12 Oct 2026. The price rose from ₹606.65 to ₹627.00 (+3.4%). In between it fell to ₹527.55 on 27 Oct in a sector-wide slide (Muthoot −8%, Manappuram −11% over the same days) and then recovered. Four things are new:

  1. Q2 FY27 beat. PAT before minority interest was ₹805 cr against the ₹720 cr bar. GNPA was 1.48%, gold AUM grew 13% QoQ with tonnage up 8%, and ROE was 20.8%.
  2. Targets rose. Motilal Oswal went from ₹700 to ₹760. The Trendlyne consensus went from ₹730 to ₹754 (range ₹650–₹815) and FY27 EPS from ₹63.2 to ₹68.7.
  3. The September shareholding steadied the FII line (23.86% to 25.63%).
  4. The ₹6,414.2 cr preferential issue (detailed below).

The rating stays Buy. The 12 Oct "Add more aggressively" trigger was met on 15 Oct (see triggers). One correction to the earlier notes: the Q2 deck puts FY27 guidance at ~25% AUM growth and 16–20% ROE ("equity raise amount will have impact"), not ~25% ROE and 16–20% AUM growth as carried before.

The evidence. PAT has now risen five quarters running: ₹418 cr, ₹501 cr, ₹623 cr, ₹713 cr, ₹805 cr. Spread held at 7.4% (yield 16.50%, cost of borrowing 9.08%). Credit cost fell to ₹281 cr, about 0.94% of average AUM annualisedd. The street's FY27 EPS of ₹68.7 is roughly H1 EPS of ₹33.9 doubled, so it still assumes no further improvement. The raise is priced at the SEBI floor, not at a discount to it. It comes with a large global sponsor on the board and promoters adding ₹1,264 cr of their own money. Both matter at a lender that was banned from new gold loans in 2024.

The counter-argument. Gold is now 53% of AUM, and stress is visible inside it. Gold GNPA rose from 0.61% to 0.90%. On the call an analyst said about 7% of the gold book is 1–30 days past due. Loans already in NPA carry LTV near 85%. Nirmal Jain said a sudden gold fall could push gold NPAs to 1–3%, though losses should stay small. The raise also means two to four quarters of mechanically lower ROE (about 14–16%d on the enlarged equity) while the money is lent out. A market expecting a premium-to-market strategic deal may read ₹602 as "at the floor". For a holder: keep holding. Adding near the ₹602 issue price is reasonable on any weakness when trading reopens on 11 Nov. Do not chase it above about ₹650 (1.6x pro-forma book) before the EGM on 4 Dec.

The ₹6,414 cr preferential issue · board approval 10 Nov 2026

The board met on 10 Nov from 11:15 to 14:00, after a 5 Nov intimation. It approved a ₹6,414.2 cr primary raise on a private-placement basis. The money comes in two instruments: compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS) for the outside investors and warrants for the promoters, both at ₹602. The outcome was filed at 15:59 IST and the press release at 16:33, on an exchange holiday.

TermDetail
Who is the investorBlackstone private equity: a financial investor, not a strategic one. It is not a promoter and not Fairfax. FIH Mauritius (Fairfax) held 13.64% at Sep-26, takes no part in the issue, and is diluted to 10.91%d. The investors are classified as public shareholders and are unrelated to the promoters.
Price₹602 per share, a 4.0% discount to the ₹627 close on 9 Nov and 0.8% below the ₹606.65 price in the 12 Oct note. That is 1.66x Sep-26 book of ₹362 (1.71x the company's ₹351.5).
SEBI floorThe filing says the price is "not lower than the floor" under ICDR Reg 164(1), read with Reg 166A. Reg 166A applies because Blackstone takes more than 5%, so an independent valuer's report is also required. The press release calls ₹602 "in line with the regulatory price". The floor figure itself is not disclosed. On Yahoo daily data, with 4 Nov as the relevant date (30 days before the EGM), the 90-day VWAP is ≈₹600d and the 10-day VWAP ≈₹554d. ₹602 therefore sits at the floor: no premium, but no discount below it either.
ConversionCCPS convert 1:1 into equity by agreement, and mandatorily at 17 months 15 days from allotment. They carry a preferential dividend on terms set in the agreements, which are not disclosed. Promoter warrants are exercisable in tranches within 18 months. 25% is paid upfront (₹316 cr) and 75% on exercise (₹948 cr). An unexercised warrant lapses and the upfront money is forfeited.
Cash timing₹5,466 crd arrives at allotment (all of the CCPS money plus 25% of the warrants). The remaining ₹948 cr arrives only if the promoters exercise.
Dilution10.65 cr new shares on 42.68 cr pre-issue shares: +25.0% to the share count, or 20.0% of the post-issue fully diluted base of 53.34 crd. That is at the top of the "10–20% dilution" Nirmal Jain signalled on the 15 Oct call.
Control / open offerThe company states there is no change in control. Promoters remain promoters, at 23.75%d fully diluted (from 24.76%). Blackstone at 15.57% is below the 25% open-offer threshold in SAST Reg 3(1), so no open offer is triggered on the fully diluted numbers. unresolved Sequencing matters: if the promoters exercised all their warrants before the CCPS converted, the promoter group would briefly hold 28.3%d of voting equity, above 25%. The filing does not address this. In practice it is managed by timing the conversion first, but it is worth checking in the EGM notice.
Blackstone's rightsWith about 10% or more, it can nominate 2 board directors. With about 7.5% or more, it can nominate 1 director plus 1 on IIFL Home Finance and 2 on IIFL Samasta. It also gets customary reserved-matter vetoes and a pre-emptive right on future issues. These require a Reg 31B special resolution. The company describes them as "customary protective rights".
Use of proceedsPrimary capital into the standalone NBFC (the gold book sits there). The stated aims are capital adequacy, a lower cost of funds (possible rating upgrades) and faster gold-loan growth. A detailed objects clause will be in the EGM notice, which is not yet filed.
Lock-inNot stated in the filing. The ICDR Reg 167 defaults: promoter warrants and the shares from them are locked in for 18 months; non-promoter CCPS and the conversion shares for 6 months, both from trading approval. The promoters' existing holdings are locked from the relevant date until 90 days after trading approval. Longer contractual lock-ins in the Blackstone agreement, if any, are not disclosed.
ApprovalsA special resolution at the EGM on 4 Dec 2026 (11:00 IST, by video conference) covering the capital increase, the MoA and AoA changes, Blackstone's special rights and the issue itself. Also required: CCI approval (named for Blackstone) and in-principle approvals from BSE and NSE. The RBI is not named. The press release says "customary regulatory approvals". RBI prior approval for NBFC ownership changes is normally triggered at 26% or more, so 15.6% should not need it, but this is not confirmed in the filing.
Also at the EGMCo-promoter R. Venkataraman is re-designated from Joint MD to Non-Executive Non-Independent Director.

Market reaction

None yet. 10 Nov was a holiday, and 11 Nov trades after this note. Before the news, the stock had already risen 18.8% from ₹527.65 (30 Oct) to ₹627 (9 Nov). The whole sector rose with it (Muthoot +21%, Manappuram +12%, Nifty +10.8% from its 23 Oct low), so none of that is a deal premium. On 9 Nov, ₹627 sits 4% above the issue price. A move back toward ₹602 on 11 Nov would mean the market reads "at the floor" as a disappointment. A move above ₹650 would mean it values the governance and funding-cost upside more. Neither is known yet. No broker note on the issue was found as of 10 Nov evening.

Deep dive · added 13 Oct 2026 at ₹603 · updated 10 Nov 2026 at ₹627

The 13 Oct call was "keep holding, don't add before 15 Oct". The print then cleared all three add conditions. On 13 Oct, expected upside of 20–25% against about 20% downside looked fair rather than compelling. The capital raise changes that balance in two ways. It lifts the bear-case book value per share by about 13%, which raises the floor under the price. It also lowers the upside multiple for the next year, because ROE dips while the money is lent out.

2 · Asset quality and the gold book

  • Mix. Gold is 52.9% of AUM (₹65,729 cr), up from 51%. Home loans are 27.2%, MSME 9.6% and microfinance 7.6%. The secured share is 90%. Off-book loans (assignment plus co-lending) are ₹42,796 cr, about 34% of AUM, with 18 bank partners.
  • The gold book is still clean, but less so. Gold GNPA rose from 0.61% to 0.90% after gold fell late in the quarter. Loans already in NPA carry LTV near 85%. Management expects losses to stay small because the collateral covers them. Non-gold quality improved: MSME GNPA fell from 3.67% to 3.03% and microfinance from 3.39% to 2.90%. Run-off of the discontinued unsecured book continued (₹2,507 cr to ₹2,008 cr).
  • Rules are uniform. The RBI gold-loan directions have applied to all lenders since 1 Apr 2026, with LTV caps of 85%, 80% and 75% by ticket size. Management also flagged a draft consultation on insurance cross-selling norms as an industry fee-income risk.
  • Track record. The RBI stopped new gold loans from March to September 2024. A Blackstone nominee board and reserved-matter rights add outside oversight. That is a modest but real offset to the regulatory tail.

Business

Nature of business

A diversified retail NBFC with ₹1,24,268 cr of AUM across 5,091 branches. The mix is gold loans 53% (₹65,729 cr, 69.3 tonnes, average ticket ₹1.2 lakh), home loans 27% (IIFL Home Finance, CRAR 39%), secured MSME 9.6%, microfinance 7.6% (IIFL Samasta) and a ₹2,008 cr unsecured book in run-off. The gold business sits in the standalone parent, whose 17% CRAR and 12.5% Tier I were the binding constraint, which is why the new equity goes there. About 34% of AUM is off balance sheet. Funding includes $800 mn of offshore bonds raised in H1.

Moat

Narrow. The gold-loan branch network, appraisal and vaulting take years and regulatory trust to build, which is a real barrier. Muthoot and Manappuram are larger in gold, though, and banks compete hard for secured gold credit. The other lines are commoditised retail lending. The edge is execution and cost of funds. The Blackstone capital and a possible rating upgrade bear directly on cost of funds, the one lever IIFL had least of.

Confirmed order book: not applicable to a lender. The forward-visibility equivalents are AUM guidance (~25% FY27) and ₹13,805 cr of liquidity.

Risks

A regulatory repeat on gold loans still breaks the thesis. Gold is now 53% of AUM (₹65,700 cr). A second RBI action would hit most of the earnings, and the stock would de-rate below book, deal or no deal.
  • Gold-book stress is building. Gold GNPA rose 29 bps to 0.90%. About 7% of the gold book is 1–30 days past due (per an analyst on the call), and loans in NPA carry LTV near 85%. A 10% gold fall could take gold NPAs to 1–3% (management's own range).
  • Dilution before deployment. EPS falls 12–20% and ROE drops to about 14–16%d until the ₹6,414 cr is lent out. The consensus has not yet adjusted.
  • Deal execution. The deal needs a special resolution, CCI approval and exchange approvals. The CCPS dividend terms and lock-ins are undisclosed. The SAST sequencing of warrants versus CCPS is unaddressed. The promoter warrants (₹948 cr) are optional: 75% of that money arrives only if the promoters exercise.
  • Overhangs. Fairfax's remaining 10.9%, and Blackstone's own exit in 4–6 years, are future supply.
  • Growth above guidance. AUM grew 14.9% in H1 alone, with gold +90% YoY on a high gold price. Growth at this speed is how 2024 started.
