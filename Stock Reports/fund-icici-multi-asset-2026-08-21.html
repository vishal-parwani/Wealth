India's largest and one of the best-regarded multi-asset funds, run by CIO S. Naren since 2012. It blends equities (with a contrarian value tilt), debt, gold, silver and arbitrage into a single moderate-risk vehicle that has compounded ~18% over 5 years with materially lower drawdowns than pure equity. A genuine one-fund core holding — the reason the new Active FoF exists is to package this kind of allocation.

03Strategy & what it holds

Model. Actively allocates across asset classes by valuation: equities (large-cap tilt, contrarian), debt (accrual + duration), gold & silver (via ETFs/derivatives), and a meaningful arbitrage/derivatives sleeve — the last is deliberate, keeping net-equity high enough to retain equity taxation while managing risk.

Equity (net + arb) ~65–80%Debt ~10–25%Gold/Silver ~10–15%

Naren actively cuts equity into froth and adds in fear — the fund's defining behaviour and the source of its low-drawdown record.

04Cost & suitability
  • Cost. Direct expense ~0.54%* — low for an actively managed hybrid; cheaper than layering a FoF on top.
  • Tax. Structured to qualify for equity taxation via the arbitrage sleeve (verify current status) — a real edge vs debt-taxed multi-asset FoFs.
  • Who it's for. A core holding for moderate-risk investors wanting one-fund diversification with downside cushioning; excellent SIP anchor.
05Risks
  • Size. ~₹84,000 Cr can limit nimbleness in mid/small-caps (mitigated by its large-cap tilt).
  • Equity-heavy. Despite the "multi-asset" label, net-equity is high — it will fall in a deep equity bear market, just less than pure equity.
  • Commodity timing. Gold/silver calls add or subtract in any given year.
  • Manager/key-person. Heavily associated with S. Naren — watch for any transition.
