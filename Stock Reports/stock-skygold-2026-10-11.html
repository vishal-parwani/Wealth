Verdict

The operating story is the strongest of the three names from this tweet: growth, rising margins and, at last, cash. But at 42.6x trailing and 12x book, after a tripling, the price is ahead of what has been proven. Hold.

For. Revenue grew 77% in FY26 to ₹6,292 cr, and PAT grew 112% to ₹282 cr. Q1 FY27 kept the pace: revenue ₹2,013 cr (+78%) and PAT ₹105 cr (+141%). EBITDA margin has risen every quarter since September 2024, from 5.1% to 7.8%. The value-added share of the mix went from under 10% in FY23 to 50–55%. Two levers are now starting to show:

  • "Advance gold", where the customer supplies the metal and Sky Gold bills only the making charge, rose from 5.7% of volume to 17%.
  • Q1 operating cash flow was +₹30 cr, the first positive figure after −₹158 cr, −₹272 cr and −₹45 cr in FY24–FY26.

Guidance was raised to ₹8,100 cr revenue for FY27 with a 4.5–4.75% PAT margin. Q1's run-rate is already about there. On governance, a BDO-network auditor (MSKA) replaced V J Shah & Co. in May, and the promoters say they will take no salary from FY27, only dividends.

Against. The stock is ₹921, 3.2x its 52-week low. BOB Capital, the most active broker on the name, moved to Hold with a ₹749 target in August when the stock was ₹780. The stock has since risen another 18%. On guidance, FY27 EPS is about ₹24–25, so you are paying 37–39x a year that has not happened yet, for a 5%-margin jewellery fabricator. Real holders are trimming and momentum funds are buying. In August Bandhan Small Cap, Motilal Small Cap, Motilal Consumption and BOI Flexi Cap all sold. Meanwhile three "active momentum" schemes opened new positions, Motilal's at 6.35% of the fund. Momentum buyers sell when the trend breaks. Two things will take more than one quarter to judge. A wholly owned subsidiary disclosed a cyber-fraud loss of up to ₹10.7 cr after Q1. And the outgoing auditor's reasons for stepping down were not stated in the filing.

Testing the tweet. On 20 Sep 2026 @TheAlpha10X put Sky Gold on a 63-stock "High Growth + High Quality" list for buying the dip. High growth: confirmed, and emphatically: revenue +77% and PAT +112% in FY26, and +78% and +141% in Q1. High quality: partly confirmed. ROCE is 27% and ROE 29%, and margins are rising. But the company burned ₹475 cr of operating cash over FY24–26, and only one quarter of positive cash flow sits against that. The dip: there isn't one. The stock was ₹783.70 at the close before the tweet (18 Sep). It is ₹921 now, up 17.5% and within 2% of its high.

Business

Nature of business

Sky Gold designs and makes lightweight 22-carat gold jewellery: chains, mangalsutras, bangles and casting, electroforming and stamped pieces. It sells business to business to India's organised jewellery chains and, increasingly, to the Middle East. It has 14.4 tonnes a year of capacity at a single Navi Mumbai location and about 1,500 staff, including 180 designers. Exports were about 18% of Q1 revenue. Revenue includes the gold itself, except in the advance-gold model, where only the making charge is billed. That is why the margin percentage is small and moves with the gold price.

Moat

Narrow. Design depth (900,000+ catalogue designs) and turnaround speed (7–20 days) make Sky Gold a preferred vendor to the large chains. But the chains multi-source, hold pricing power, and could take manufacturing in-house. Scale is the real edge, and it is not unique.

Order book

Not a meaningful concept beyond the ₹30–45 cr of initial export interest from the London expo. Revenue is driven by retailer replenishment.

Risks

Valuation after a tripling, held up by momentum money. At 42.6x trailing and 12x book, against a closest peer at 16x, any slowdown toward the gold-jewellery industry's normal growth would compress the multiple sharply. The new momentum-fund holders are the first to leave on a break.
  • Gold price. High prices hurt jewellery volumes and tie up more working capital. A sharp fall would deflate reported revenue.
  • Working capital and debt. Net debt is ₹634 cr. The cash turnaround rests on one quarter.
  • Controls. A ₹10.7 cr cyber-fraud loss at a subsidiary, and an auditor exit without stated reasons, in the same year.
  • Customer concentration. A handful of large chains, each able to multi-source.
  • Acquisition integration. Several bolt-ons so far, and controls at acquired units are already in question.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: H1 FY27 operating cash flow above ₹100 cr, a first dividend, and a price nearer 28–30x FY27E (about ₹700).
  • Downgrade to Sell: operating cash flow turning negative again, a qualified audit opinion, or promoter selling resuming.
