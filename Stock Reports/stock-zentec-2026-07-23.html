FY26 was a deliberately "muted" year (revenue and PAT down YoY on order-timing) — the stock fell ~14% on results — but it exits with a record ₹1,336 Cr order book covering 3–4 years. 02Business & Qualitative

Model. Designs and builds defence training & simulation systems (driving, gunnery, live-range, tactical), counter-drone / anti-drone systems, remote/automated weapon stations, combat robotics and drones — primarily for the Indian armed forces and paramilitary, with growing exports. Hyderabad-based, founder-led (Chairman & MD Ashok Atluri).

Moat. Deep IP and a long-entrenched position in simulators (high switching costs once embedded in armed-forces training), plus a first-mover edge in indigenous counter-drone — a category pulled forward by recent conflicts. Five distinct capability lines now broaden the addressable market.

Quality markers. 5-year revenue CAGR ~47%; high gross margins; net-cash; consistent profitability (unlike most defence small-caps). Management explicitly framed FY26 as a transition year, prioritising portfolio breadth over near-term numbers.
