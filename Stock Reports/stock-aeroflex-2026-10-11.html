Verdict

The liquid-cooling story is real, but the price has run past even the most bullish published estimate. Sell. The only broker covering the stock, ICICI Securities, has a target of ₹545, 5% above spot. On its own FY28 numbers, the stock trades at about 61xd earnings two years out. At ₹520 the market is paying 103x trailing and about 87xd FY27 for a business guiding to 30–35% growth, with a 14% ROE and a ROCE that has fallen every year from 34% to 19%.

What has genuinely changed. Aeroflex makes SFN skid assemblies — the manifolds and hoses that carry coolant to AI-server racks. These brought in ₹32.4 cr in Q1 FY27, 22–23% of revenue. Capacity rose from 6,000 to 9,000 skids a year on 1 July and is targeted at 15,000 by Q3 FY27. Q1 revenue was ₹145 cr (+73%) and profit ₹19 cr (+171% on Screener, +162% as reported). The balance sheet is net cash. This is a well-run export manufacturer that has found a fast-growing niche.

The tweet, tested. @TheAlpha10X included AEROFLEX in a 63-stock "high growth + high quality" watchlist "for buying the dip" (20 Sep 2026). High growth: refuted for FY26 — revenue +18%, profit +6%. It is confirmed only for the latest quarter, and that came against a weak June 2025 base (PAT ₹7 cr, −42% YoY). High quality: partly — net cash and steady 23% margins, but ROCE has halved in three years and free cash flow was negative in FY25 and FY26. The dip: an 11% pullback from ₹584, after a 3.3x run from ₹158. That is a pause rather than a dip.

The register is also moving the wrong way for a long-term holder. DIIs went from 3.80% to 1.48% in the June quarter. LIC MF Small Cap and Union Active Momentum both exited in August. Only TrustMF is adding. FII and retail money is now carrying the price.

Business

Nature of business

The company was incorporated in 1993 and makes stainless-steel flexible flow products: corrugated and braided hoses, hose assemblies, expansion bellows and related fittings for industrial, HVAC, energy and process customers. About 69% of FY26 revenue was exports. The new leg is the liquid-cooling SFN skid assembly — the manifold-and-hose unit that distributes coolant across AI-server racks in data centres. It was ₹32.4 cr in Q1 FY27, about 22% of revenue, and capacity is being taken from 9,000 to 15,000 units a year by Q3 FY27.

Moat

Narrow. Product approvals with export OEMs and a long record in stainless-steel hoses give it steady customers and a reliable 20%-plus margin. Liquid-cooling skids, however, are fabricated assemblies built to specifications set by the server or cooling-system vendor. Other fabricators can make them, and the customer holds the design. Speed and capacity are an advantage today. They are not a lasting barrier.

Order book

Not disclosed. The company reports capacity (units a year) and segment revenue, not a confirmed order book or named liquid-cooling customers. Capacity growing faster than disclosed demand is a gap in the evidence.

Risks

Multiple compression. At 103x trailing earnings, delivering the 30–35% growth guidance still leaves the stock at about 87x FY27. A return to 40x FY28's derived EPS of ₹8.6 is ₹344, 34% below today. The stock has tripled on a theme, and themes get re-priced quickly when AI capex sentiment turns.
  • Customer concentration in liquid cooling. No customers are named. A single hyperscaler supply chain could account for most skid revenue.
  • Returns are falling. ROCE went from 34% to 26% to 22% to 19% as capacity ran ahead of profit. Doubling skid capacity repeats that pattern.
  • Institutions are leaving. DII holding halved in the June quarter, and two of four fund holders exited in August.
  • Group structure. A sister listed company is entering the same theme, so related-party flows need watching.
  • Export exposure. About 69% of revenue is exports, exposed to US and EU demand and tariffs.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Hold: the price falls to about ₹390, roughly 45x derived FY28 EPS. Or H1 FY27 shows liquid cooling above 30% of revenue with operating margin above 25%, which would make ICICI's FY28 numbers look conservative.
  • Upgrade to Buy: a price near ₹300, about 35x FY28, with named liquid-cooling customers or a disclosed multi-year supply agreement.
