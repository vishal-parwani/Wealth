Verdict

Nothing has changed except the price, which fell. TechD is an SME listing and reports twice a year, so the second half of FY26 (published in May) is still the latest result. The full year was strong: revenue ₹53 cr (+79%), operating margin 36%, profit ₹14 cr (+68%), and no debt. Management guides ₹75–80 cr organic revenue for FY27 and ₹100 cr+ including planned acquisitions. The half-year to September, published by mid-November, is the first check on that guidance.

The stock is ₹583, down from ₹660 in July, and 31x FY26 earnings. If the organic guidance is met at a similar margin, that falls to about 20xd. The price drop has made it more reasonable, but two things hold it back. First, customers took 136 days to pay in FY26 (154 days in FY25). For a services company that invoices monthly, that's slow, and FY26 operating cash flow was ₹9 cr against ₹14 cr of profit. Second, there's no reliable price history or volume. Yahoo has almost no trading data for the SME symbol. The July note's buy level was "low ₹400s". Hold, high risk.

Business

What it does

Formerly TechDefence Labs. A CERT-In empanelled, ISO 27001-certified cybersecurity services firm: security audits, penetration testing, compliance work for RBI and SEBI rules, managed security operations, and training. Clients are in banking and finance, NBFCs, fintech, telecom, manufacturing, healthcare, aviation, IT and government. International revenue rose to 21% by H1 FY26, from 15%.

Peers

The comparison is the other two Kedia-backed cyber names in this library. TAC Infosec (rated Buy, 21 Aug) sells its own software products at higher margins. Exato (Hold, high risk) does AI customer-experience work. TechD is the pure services business of the three: the lowest multiple, and the least protected by a product of its own. No large listed Indian cybersecurity peer is directly comparable.

Moat

Thin to moderate. CERT-In empanelment and regulator-driven audit demand are a real barrier for small new entrants, but not against the Big-4 or large IT firms. Services grow with headcount, not software. The move into managed security (recurring monthly contracts) is the route to something more durable.

Order book

No order book is disclosed. Announced orders are small (₹1–2 cr). FY27 guidance of ₹75–80 cr organic revenue is the only forward commitment.

Risks

Almost no market to sell into. SME lots, 1,677 shareholders and very little recorded trading. The stock has traded between ₹360 and ₹844 in a year. Getting out in size may not be possible.
  • Slow collections: 136 debtor days. Profit is not yet cash.
  • Acquisitions to reach the ₹100 cr target: for a ₹50 cr company, buying another business is a big execution risk.
  • People: a services model depends on hiring and keeping scarce security staff.
  • Half-yearly reporting means long gaps without information.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: H1 FY27 revenue at or above ₹35 cr (on track for ₹75 cr+) with debtor days below 110. Or a price in the low ₹400s.
  • Downgrade to Sell: H1 growth below 30%, receivables stretching past 160 days, or an expensive acquisition paid for with shares.
