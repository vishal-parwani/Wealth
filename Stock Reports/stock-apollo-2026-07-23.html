A Hyderabad defence-electronics small-cap in full multibagger mode (~+99% in a year; ~36x over five). FY26 was a "breakthrough" year — revenue +61%, PAT roughly doubled — as production orders converted at scale and the company diversified into explosives, UAVs and naval mines. The numbers are inflecting; the valuation (~120x) and negative operating cash flow are the offsets.

02Business & Qualitative

Model. Designs and manufactures mission-critical, time-sensitive defence electronics — embedded systems, electronic-warfare suites, electro-optic systems and weapon/missile subsystems — for the Ministry of Defence, DRDO and defence PSUs, on a "total solutions under one roof" model. Near-three-decade track record; MD Baddam Karunakar Reddy.

Moat / positioning. Entrenched as a build-to-spec partner on indigenous missile and space programmes — sticky once designed-in. FY26 widened the moat into adjacent high-barrier categories: it became the only Indian company to develop Limpet mines for the Navy, acquired IDL Explosives (via ADIPL), and secured a DPIIT UAV-production licence and a first export order.

Diversification thesis. Management is steering Apollo from a components/subsystems supplier toward a diversified defence OEM (naval mines, explosives, missiles, UAVs), with a further acquisition flagged for next year.

05Valuation

At ~120x trailing earnings and ~11x book, the stock prices in several years of sustained high growth with essentially no margin of safety. The bull math relies on FY27–28 earnings compounding fast enough to grow into the multiple; the bear math notes that low ROE + negative operating cash flow make that compounding lower-quality than the headline P&L suggests. Any order-inflow or execution disappointment risks a sharp de-rating.
