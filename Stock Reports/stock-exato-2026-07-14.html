Verdict

Real growth, but the price just ran away. Exato is a genuinely fast-growing AI/CX software microcap — FY26 revenue up 35% to ₹168 cr, PAT up 67% to ₹16.1 cr, and a claimed ₹600 cr order book (~3.6x FY26 revenue). But the trigger for this report is also the trap: after Vijay Kedia's bulk purchase at ₹429 became public (9 Jul), the stock went vertical — up ~68% in five straight sessions to ₹610, an all-time high. You would be paying ~42% above the price a famously patient investor just paid. The business merits a spot on the watchlist; the entry does not. Wait for the Kedia-momentum froth to settle.

Update · 23 Jul 2026: No fresh trade print since 13 Jul (₹610.05); new software-license export order from Diligenta (UK). Still trading well above Vijay Kedia's ₹429 entry — froth intact. Rating unchanged (Speculative / Wait for Pullback).

Business

Exato operates at the intersection of AI-as-a-Service and Customer-Experience-as-a-Service. Proprietary platforms: ExatoIQ, Prompt Base Dialer, UAM, and CompliCall. Exports were 25% of FY26 revenue with a stated target of 60% in 2–3 years; subsidiaries in the USA, Singapore, and (since Mar 2026) Australia. On 1 Jul 2026 it announced a $479k software-license export order. Recent leadership hires: a Chief AI Officer and a COO (both effective 1 Jun 2026).

Why Kedia is in (the bull case)

  • Order book of ₹600 cr against ₹168 cr FY26 revenue gives multi-year revenue visibility if it converts.
  • PAT growing ~2x faster than revenue (67% vs 35%) — operating leverage kicking in.
  • Kedia and PACs kept adding through 2026: 3.48% → 5.45% → 9.68%, including a ~₹4.3 cr bulk deal at ₹429 in July. This is conviction sizing, not a punt.
  • AI + CX is among the highest-growth digital-transformation segments, and Exato has actual paying export customers, not just a narrative.

Risks

Price action risk dominates. +68% in five sessions on a celebrity-investor disclosure is classic SME-style froth; these moves round-trip more often than not. The stock is at its all-time high with zero technical support between ₹610 and the low-₹400s.
  • Microcap / liquidity: ~₹612 cr market cap; exits are hard in a down move; circuit filters can lock you in.
  • Order-book quality unverified: ₹600 cr is a company-stated figure from a press release; conversion timelines and margins are not disclosed. Verify against annual-report disclosures.
  • Modest margins for "AI": 15% EBITDA margin looks more like a services/BPO profile than a software-product one — the AI-platform framing may flatter the mix.
  • Related-party / governance depth: typical microcap disclosure limitations; limited analyst coverage; numbers here come from company releases and financial media, not audited filings I have reviewed.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Accumulate: a pullback toward ₹430–480 (Kedia's own entry zone), or FY27 H1 results confirming order-book conversion at stable margins.
  • Downgrade to Avoid: order-book conversion slipping, export growth stalling, or promoter/PAC selling into the rally.
