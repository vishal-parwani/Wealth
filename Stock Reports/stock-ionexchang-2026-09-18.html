Verdict

Downgrade to Sell. The earnings reset got worse, and the price hasn't reflected it. Q1 FY27 revenue rose 20% to ₹701 cr, but EBITDA halved to ₹32 cr and profit fell 94% to ₹3.1 cr. Margin was 4.5%. Old water-treatment projects (a UP government job, a Kuwait plant) are still losing money. That segment made a ₹17 cr loss on ₹210 cr of revenue, and management says the UP project will run into next year. The July note also wrongly assumed the specialty chemicals business was safe. Its profit fell 52% because of geopolitical disruption, and the new Roha resin plant is running at only about 25% of capacity in its first year and adding depreciation.

Management won't give a timeline for margin recovery ("quietly confident… over time"). Trailing profit has fallen from ₹208 cr in FY25 to ₹98 cr. Free cash flow was about −₹280 cr in each of the last two years. A net cash position of ₹436 cr two years ago is now ₹163 cr of net debt. The stock fell 11% to ₹372 on results day, then recovered to ₹442, above its pre-results price, at 59x trailing earnings.

The July note said Buy at ₹350–370 if things steadied, and Sell on "another guide-down, receivables deterioration, or order-book conversion slipping". Two of those have happened: there's no guidance, and projects are late. The long-term case is still fine: water scarcity, a ₹2,473 cr order book, a ₹9,777 cr bid pipeline. But at this price you are paying a recovery multiple for earnings that are still falling. Sell.

Business

What it does

India's original water-treatment company, founded in 1964. Specialty Chemicals: ion-exchange resins and membranes, exported and competing with Dow and Purolite. This is the high-margin franchise, now with the new Roha plant. Treatment Solutions: designing and building water, wastewater, zero-discharge and desalination plants for refineries, power, pharma and municipalities. Lumpy, and the current problem. Industrial Products and Lifecycle Services (operations and maintenance, spares): recurring and steady. Consumer: Zero B home water purifiers.

Moat

Real in chemicals and services. Resin and membrane know-how, 60 years of plant references, and a large installed base that needs spares and upkeep. None of that has disappeared. Project work (Treatment Solutions) has no moat: fixed-price bids where losses on one big job wipe out a year's profit, which is what's happening now.

Order book

₹2,473 cr at 30 June 2026 (company), about 0.8x trailing revenue, plus the ₹440 cr Hyundai E&C win after the quarter. Bids in progress ₹9,777 cr. How big the order book is isn't the issue. The issue is the margin on it. The loss-making legacy jobs are part of it and will stay in the numbers into FY28.

Risks · to the Sell call

A quick recovery would prove the Sell wrong. If the legacy projects finish and chemicals margin recovers as Roha fills, profit could return to FY25's ₹208 cr. That would put today's price at about 31x, which is reasonable for this franchise. Abakkus is betting on exactly that. The Sell is a view that it will take longer than the price allows.
  • For holders: no margin guidance, another year of legacy losses, and falling interest income on less cash.
  • Roha: the biggest investment the company has made, running at about 25% in year one with "softer than expected" demand. Heavy depreciation hits before any matching profit.
  • Working capital: operating cash flow was negative in FY26. Delayed large projects will keep cash tied up.
  • Specialty chemicals exposed to geopolitics: export resin volumes and pricing were hit in Q1 and could stay weak.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Hold: quarterly EBITDA margin back above 8%, with specialty chemicals EBIT growing year on year. Or the price back near ₹350 (the July buy zone), which would price in a slower recovery.
  • Upgrade to Buy: two quarters of margin in double digits and positive operating cash flow for the half-year, showing the legacy losses are behind it.
