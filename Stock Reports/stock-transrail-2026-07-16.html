Accumulate on Dips

Verdict

A growth EPC at a value multiple, mid-correction. Transrail delivered a strong FY26 — revenue +30% to ₹6,880 cr, operating PAT +28% to ₹421 cr, operating cash flow nearly doubling to ₹817 cr with net debt reduced — and carries a record ₹16,361 cr unexecuted order book (~2.4x FY26 revenue, 61% domestic / 39% international). Yet the stock has fallen ~41% from its 52-week high of ₹856 to ₹508, leaving it at roughly 16x trailing earnings — cheap against the T&D capex cycle (KEC, Kalpataru trade richer) and against its own growth rate. The blemish is Q4 FY26: revenue -4% and operating PAT -24% YoY, a reminder that EPC quarters are lumpy and margins can wobble on execution mix. The de-rating has largely priced that in; the order book has not stopped growing (₹575 cr fresh T&D wins plus ₹400 cr L1 in July; ₹459 cr overseas orders in late June). Accumulate on weakness rather than all at once — a soft Q1 print could offer better levels.

Update · 23 Jul 2026: Price roughly flat (~₹510–520). Bagged fresh ₹575 cr orders and guides ₹10,000–11,000 cr of new orders this year on a ₹16,361 cr book. Governance watch: Deputy MD Raman Rajagopalan resigned, effective 31 Jul 2026 (personal reasons). Rating unchanged (Accumulate on Dips).

Business

Four-decade-old EPC company (formerly part of Gammon) focused on power transmission & distribution — turnkey lines, substations — with backward-integrated manufacturing of lattice towers, conductors, and monopoles. Smaller verticals: railway electrification, poles & lighting, and civil works (bridges, tunnels, cooling towers). International footprint across ~60 countries, with overseas projects now 39% of the order book — Bangladesh, Africa, and the Middle East feature prominently in wins.

Bull case

  • Multi-year T&D capex cycle: India's renewable-integration transmission build-out (interstate grid, green-energy corridors) underwrites tender flow for years; Transrail's ₹8,520 cr FY26 inflows show it is winning share.
  • Revenue visibility: ₹16,361 cr book ≈ 2.4 years of revenue; management guided to sustained 25–30% growth on the FY26 earnings call.
  • Cash conversion improving: OCF ₹817 cr (+96%) with net debt down ₹80 cr — rare discipline in EPC, where working capital usually eats growth.
  • Valuation support: ~16x trailing vs larger peers at materially higher multiples, despite comparable growth and backward integration (towers + conductors in-house protects margin vs pure contractors).

Risks

Q4 FY26 stumble. Revenue −4% and operating PAT −24% YoY in the March quarter shows execution/margin lumpiness; if Q1 FY27 repeats it, the growth narrative (and multiple) takes another leg down.
  • Government/PSU concentration: ~70% of revenue historically from government tenders — exposed to policy shifts, tender delays, and payment cycles; top-10 clients were ~67% of revenue around listing.
  • Commodity pass-through: steel, zinc, aluminium swings squeeze fixed-price contracts.
  • International execution risk: 39% overseas order book includes frontier markets (currency, geopolitics, receivables).
  • EPC competition: aggressive bidding from KEC, Kalpataru, Skipper and Chinese players can compress win margins across the cycle.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: Q1/Q2 FY27 restoring >20% PAT growth with EBITDA margin back above ~12.5%, or price near ₹430–450 (IPO-price zone) without fundamental deterioration.
  • Downgrade to Watch/Avoid: consecutive weak quarters, order-inflow drought, working-capital stretch reversing the FY26 cash-flow gains, or major overseas receivable trouble.
