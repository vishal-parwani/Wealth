Verdict

Hold. Don't buy at ₹575. The rating moves up from Sell because the earnings rose, not because the price fell. H1 FY27 revenue was ₹353 cr, up 81%. Management's guidance for the full year was 30–35%. Liquid-cooling skid volume rose 42% in a quarter to 1,475 units. The core hose and assembly business grew about 40% too, so the growth is broad-based. The 11 Oct note's valuation case rested on ICICI's FY28 EPS of about ₹8.6d. A run-rate scenario now gives about ₹12.4d, and at ₹575 the stock trades at about 46xd that. Its own "Upgrade to Hold" line was 45x FY28. A 46x multiple is fair for 40%-plus growth. It is not cheap enough to buy against the risks below.

Why not Buy. (1) Accounting flattery. From 1 April the company switched depreciation from WDV to straight-line and booked the whole half-year effect in Q2. That cut Q2 depreciation from ₹8.86 cr to ₹0.45 cr and added ₹8.23 cr to PAT, about 22% of the ₹36.6 cr reported. Like-for-like on the old method, Q2 PAT was about ₹28.3 crd, up 99% rather than 157%. The company's own clean straight-line figure is ₹34.0 cr. (2) Capacity is running ahead of demand. Q2 skid volume annualises to about 5,900 unitsd against 12,000 installed. Utilisation was 69% in June on a smaller base. The board has now approved 30,000 units by March 2028. (3) Still no named liquid-cooling customer and no order book. (4) Cash. Receivables rose 57% in six months, from ₹130 cr to ₹204 cr. H1 operating cash flow was ₹38 cr against ₹55 cr of PAT. Net cash fell from ₹69 cr to ₹28 cr, and the new ₹202 cr capex names equity and a bridge loan as funding sources. (5) The one broker's target, ICICI's ₹545, is below spot.

What changed since 11 Oct 2026. The price rose 10.5% from ₹520.50 to ₹575.00, with a large round trip in between. It rallied ahead of the 14 Oct board meeting: +9% on 12 Oct on 1.29 cr shares. It hit a record ₹613.70 intraday on 14 Oct when the ₹202 cr expansion was approved. Then it sold off 10% the next day and fell to ₹446 by 26 Oct, 27% below the high. On 29 Oct it jumped about 19% on the Q2 print, on 2.86 cr shares. New facts: (a) Q2 FY27 results, filed 28 Oct (above). (b) The 14 Oct board approved ₹124 cr to take hose capacity to 30 mn m and ₹78 cr to double skid capacity to 30,000, both by March 2028. (c) The "inferred" Q4 FY26 equity issue is now confirmed: 30.1 lakh shares were allotted on 3 Feb 2026 at ₹182.70 (₹55.0 cr) to six non-promoter investors, including Ashish Kacholia. That is 2.3% dilutiond. (d) The CFO is changing on 28 Nov, and the wholly owned subsidiary Hyd-Air is being merged in. (e) In the Sep-2026 shareholding, FIIs rose to 3.93% and DIIs held at 1.42%. No active fund bought in September; the only new holders were index funds.

For a buy/hold/sell decision. If you own it, hold. The business is now growing faster than the bull case assumed, and at 46x a run-rate FY28 the stock is no longer pricing in a fantasy. If you don't own it, wait. A ₹446–₹614 range inside a fortnight shows how sentiment-driven the price is. A pullback toward ₹450 (about 36x FY28d) or a named multi-year skid contract would make it a Buy. Sizing should reflect the high-risk tag. Business

Nature of business

Incorporated in 1993, the company makes stainless-steel flexible flow products: corrugated and braided hoses, hose assemblies, expansion bellows and fittings for industrial, HVAC, energy, ports and process customers. Exports were 60% of Q2 FY27 revenue, down from 74% a year earlier, as domestic revenue nearly tripled. The new leg is the liquid-cooling SFN skid assembly, the manifold-and-hose unit that distributes coolant across AI-server racks. Skids sell for ₹1.1–5.5 lakh each depending on specification, and they made up 24% of Q2 revenue. Capacity is 12,000 a year now, 15,000 by Dec 2026 and 30,000 by Mar 2028.

Moat

Narrow. Product approvals with export OEMs and a long record in stainless-steel hoses give it steady customers and a 20%-plus margin. Skids are fabricated to specifications set by the server or cooling-system vendor, so the customer owns the design. Rising realisations (₹3.39 lakh a unit in Q2) and 42% quarterly volume growth show Aeroflex is winning share today. Speed and capacity are the edge, not a lasting barrier.

Capacity (30,000 planned against about 5,900d annualised volume) remains ahead of disclosed demand. This is the key gap in the evidence.

Risks

Momentum and multiple. The stock fell 27% in eight sessions without any bad news, then rose 19% in one day. At 85x reported trailing earnings and about 46x a scenario FY28, any slip in skid volume or in AI-capex sentiment re-prices it fast. At 35x FY28 the price would be about ₹434d.
  • Earnings quality. The WDV-to-straight-line switch cuts depreciation by about ₹4.6 cr a quarter from here on (₹8.86 cr WDV against ₹4.27 cr straight-line in Q2). That is about ₹3.4 cr of PAT a quarter, or roughly ₹14 cr a yeard. Q2 also absorbed a ₹3.8 cr pre-tax Q1 catch-upd (₹2.6 cr after tax). Compare growth on a like-for-like basis.
  • Capacity ahead of demand. 30,000 skids planned against about 5,900 annualised. If volume stalls, the depreciation and fixed costs of a half-empty plant hit margins, as in FY26, when ROCE fell to 19%.
  • Customer concentration. No liquid-cooling customer is named, and one hyperscaler supply chain could be most of the skid revenue.
  • Working capital. Receivables rose ₹74 cr in six months, and cash conversion was 69% of PAT in H1d.
  • Dilution and funding. Equity is named for the ₹202 cr capex. Dilution is small at current prices, but it signals that accruals alone won't fund the plan.
  • Group structure. A sister listed company is entering the same theme.
  • Export exposure. About 60% of revenue is exports, exposed to US and EU demand and tariffs.
