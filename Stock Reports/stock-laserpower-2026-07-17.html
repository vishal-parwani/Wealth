Verdict

A cables play at half the peer multiple — with a question mark exactly where the discount is. Laser Power & Infra makes power cables, conductors and specialised products for the T&D industry — the same demand wave (grid capex, renewables evacuation) that powers your Transrail thesis, one layer down the supply chain. It listed yesterday at ₹250 vs the ₹214 issue price and trades at ₹262 — roughly 24x FY26 earnings against Polycab/KEI at 40x+. The discount has a reason: FY26 revenue fell ~9% to ₹2,348 cr even as PAT jumped 42% to ₹151.6 cr — a margin-driven profit spike on shrinking sales that IPO analysts (SP Tulsian, Value Research) flagged as possibly unsustainable, priced "fully" at issue. The ₹3,243 cr order book (+40% YoY, ~1.4x revenue) argues revenue re-accelerates; the ₹542 cr fresh-issue proceeds fund that growth. Sensible setup, but with two days of trading history, no public quarterly track record, and anchor lock-ins ahead, there's no rush. Watch the first two quarters as a listed company; the story gets interesting if revenue growth returns while the multiple stays sub-30x.

Update · 23 Jul 2026: ₹277.69 (22 Jul), up ~6% from listing. Only days old as a listed company — the first quarterly print is the catalyst that matters. Rating unchanged (Watch / Fresh Listing).

Business

Integrated manufacturer of power cables (LT/HT), conductors (including specialised/high-efficiency types), and related components for power transmission & distribution — selling to utilities, EPC contractors and industrial customers. Kolkata-based, with the fresh-issue capital earmarked for capacity expansion and working capital. Competes against much larger listed peers: Polycab, KEI Industries, Apar Industries, Universal Cables, Dynamic Cables.

Bull case

  • Same structural driver as the T&D EPC names: India's grid build-out needs conductors and cables in volumes the incumbents can't fully serve.
  • Order book up 40% to ₹3,243 cr gives ~1.4 years of revenue cover and points to FY27 revenue recovery.
  • Valuation gap: ~24x FY26 vs 40–60x for Polycab/KEI leaves re-rating headroom if execution proves out.
  • 41x IPO subscription and a +17–25% listing show institutional appetite for the sector.

Risks

The FY26 profit spike may be the high-water mark. PAT +42% on revenue −9% means margins did all the work — commodity (copper/aluminium) tailwinds and product mix can reverse; analysts explicitly flagged the earnings base as possibly flattered for the IPO.
  • No listed track record: two trading days old; first earnings print as a public company will set the tone violently either way.
  • Anchor/pre-IPO lock-in expiries (1–6 months out) create supply overhangs.
  • Scale disadvantage vs Polycab/KEI in distribution, brand and working-capital access; cables is a thin-margin volume game.
  • Utility/EPC receivable cycles strain working capital exactly when growth accelerates.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Accumulate: Q1/Q2 FY27 showing revenue growth returning with EBITDA margins holding, or a post-listing fade toward the ₹214–225 issue zone with the order book intact.
  • Downgrade to Avoid: margin normalisation confirming FY26 as peak earnings, or order-book conversion slipping.
