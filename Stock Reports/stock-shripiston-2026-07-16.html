SPR Auto Technologies · ex Shriram Pistons & Rings

NSE: SHRIPISTON · ~₹18,805 cr mkt cap · Abakkus holding ~₹190 cr (#2 position)
Hold

Verdict

A cash-rich ICE leader buying its way out of the EV problem — good plan, full price. The former Shriram Pistons & Rings (renamed SPR Auto Technologies, Apr 2026) dominates Indian pistons, rings and engine valves, and is deploying that cash flow into powertrain-agnostic businesses: the Antolin Group's India interiors entities, Karna Intertech, and auto interior lighting. FY26 total income rose 24.9% to ₹4,571 cr (acquisitions helping) with PAT +8.9% to ₹561 cr; post-acquisition, >35% of revenue is already ICE-agnostic, with management targeting Antolin margins doubling to 20%+ in three years and a ₹1,000 cr QIP planned to fund more. At ₹4,269 — up 67% in a year and just 3% off its ₹4,421 high — the stock trades ~35x for single-digit PAT growth this year. The re-rating already assumes the transformation succeeds. Quality compounder; wrong entry point. The 52-week low of ₹2,301 is a reminder of how much multiple risk sits under a 35x auto-component stock.

Update · 3 Aug 2026: Q1 FY27 results due 4 Aug, earnings call 5 Aug. Watch the Antolin interiors margin trajectory (guided 9–10% → 20%+ over three years) — that integration is the core of the re-rating case. Rating unchanged (Watch / Accumulate on Weakness).

Business

Market leader in pistons, piston rings, pins and engine valves (brands: SPR/Usha, honoured OEM relationships across PV/2W/3W/CV and tractors). FY26 acquisitions — Antolin India (interiors: door trims, headliners), Karna Intertech, and interior lighting JVs — are deliberately powertrain-neutral: they sell into EVs as readily as ICE. Rebranding to "SPR Auto Technologies" signals the multi-product ambition. Management guides EV penetration of only 15–17% by 2030, implying a long, profitable ICE tail funding the pivot.

Bull case

  • Core engine-parts franchise is an oligopoly with pricing power; record FY26 auto industry volumes keep it compounding.
  • Acquisition strategy converts "melting ice cube" cash flows into growth assets before the melt matters; Antolin margin-doubling is a clear, measurable target.
  • Abakkus's #2 position (~₹190 cr) endorses the capital-allocation story.
  • Balance-sheet strength plus planned ₹1,000 cr QIP funds further M&A without leverage strain.

Risks

Multiple expansion did the work. +67% in 12 months against +9% PAT growth means the last year's return was almost entirely re-rating. At ~35x/6.5x book, disappointment on Antolin integration or an auto-cycle downturn unwinds it quickly.
  • Integration risk: interiors is a lower-margin, competitive business; turning 9–10% EBITDA to 20% is ambitious.
  • QIP dilution at/near highs caps near-term EPS growth.
  • Long-term ICE decline is real even if slow; the terminal-value debate never fully goes away.

What would change the rating

  • Upgrade to Buy: pullback toward ₹3,400–3,600 (~28x), or Antolin margins visibly stepping up (>13–14%) proving the integration math.
  • Downgrade to Sell: core PAT stalling while acquisitions dilute margins — the conglomerate-discount scenario.
