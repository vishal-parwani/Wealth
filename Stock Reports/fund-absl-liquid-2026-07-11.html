Why this fund

  • Category-leading consistency. Top of the large-fund peer set on 1Y, 3Y and 5Y simultaneously — in a category where funds are separated by basis points, sustained outperformance signals genuine yield-curve and issuer-selection skill rather than luck.
  • Experienced desk. Kaustubh Gupta and Sunaina da Cunha are among the longest-serving liquid/money-market managers in India; the fund has never had a credit event.
  • Scale without bloat. ~₹47,500 cr is large enough for deep liquidity and fine institutional pricing on CDs/CPs, while remaining nimble.
  • High portfolio quality. Overwhelmingly A1+/sovereign short paper (T-bills, bank CDs, top-tier CPs) with maturity capped at 91 days by regulation.

Risks & watch items

  • Rate cycle: returns track short-term rates; with RBI in an easing phase, forward returns will drift below the 7% 3Y figure — the ~6.4–6.7% recent annualised run-rate is the better expectation.
  • Direct TER (~0.21%) is roughly double Axis Liquid's — ABSL earns it back gross of fees, but the gap is worth monitoring.
  • Liquid funds are not deposits: NAV can dip on extreme market days (Mar 2020-style), though 7-day+ holdings have essentially never lost money in this fund's history.
Use case: primary parking vehicle for surplus cash / emergency-fund tranche 1. Redemptions settle T+1; most AMCs offer ₹50k/90% instant redemption. Hold ≥7 days to avoid the graded exit load. Gains taxed at slab rate (debt taxation, post-Apr 2023 rules).
