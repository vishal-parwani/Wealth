Verdict

The most genuine "all-weather" fund among the multi-asset leaders. Second-best 3Y returns in the category (20.23% CAGR, behind only the far more aggressive Quant Multi Asset) delivered through a disciplined five-sleeve model — domestic equity, overseas equity (MSCI World), short-term debt, gold and silver — rather than momentum bets. Its worst 12-month outcome since launch has been just −2.28%, and only 0.8% of all 1-year windows were negative: category-promise behaviour, actually kept.

Why this fund

  • True diversification, not a label. Five sleeves with fixed rebalancing bands — including a ~20% MSCI World overseas allocation that no other large multi-asset peer carries at this weight. Rebalancing between uncorrelated sleeves is the return engine, not manager heroics.
  • Top-quartile returns with mid-pack risk: #2 in category on 1Y and 3Y, achieved with a max drawdown of −10.8%.
  • Specialist per sleeve: Vikram Dhawan (commodities), Vinay Sharma (equity, since Jan 2026), Sushil Budhia (debt) — a structure that fits a model-driven fund.
  • Cheap for a hybrid: 0.27% direct TER on a ₹15,000+ cr book.

Risks & watch items

  • Tax treatment is the big one. With ~50–55% domestic equity (rest overseas equity, debt, commodities), the fund does not reliably qualify for equity taxation — expect the non-equity hybrid regime: gains within 24 months at slab rate, 12.5% LTCG only after 24 months. ⚠ verify current classification in the latest factsheet/SID before a large lump sum — the AMC manages the domestic-equity band around the 65% line and treatment can differ by holding period.
  • Precious-metals tailwind: a meaningful slice of 2024–26 returns came from gold/silver; if metals mean-revert, expect the fund to lag equity-heavy peers.
  • Equity lead manager changed in Jan 2026 (Vinay Sharma) — low-impact in a model-driven fund, but new.
  • This is an equity-class holding: −8% months happen (worst 30-day −8.34%). Horizon should be 3+ years; it is not a cash substitute.
Use case: single-fund diversified core or first step out of cash into growth assets — SIP or staggered lump sum over 6–12 months. Pairs well with (rather than duplicates) ICICI Pru Multi-Asset: Nippon is model-driven with overseas + silver sleeves; ICICI is contrarian value with a domestic tilt.
