A goal-based retirement solution with four plans across the risk ladder and a 5-year (or till-retirement) lock-in. The headline Pure Equity plan has been excellent: 18.9% a year over both 3Y and 5Y, well ahead of most flexi- and multi-cap funds (Parag Parikh Flexi 12.0% 3Y, Nippon Multi Cap 12.3%). The lock-in gives no extra tax break, but it enforces the long-horizon discipline that retirement investing needs — which for most people is the whole point.

03Structure & how to use it

Mechanism. Pick a plan by horizon/risk; switch between plans as you age (switches within the fund may reset lock-in on the switched units — verify). Lock-in = 5 years or until age 60/retirement, whichever is earlier.

Young / long horizon → Pure EquityMid → Hybrid AggressiveNear retirement → Hybrid Cons / Debt
Verify — important
This is a Solution fund, not an ELSS — the lock-in does not give an 80C deduction. Its benefit is behavioural discipline, not tax. Confirm current lock-in/switch rules and taxation before investing.
04Cost, suitability & the lock-in trade
  • Cost. Direct expense ~0.66%* — reasonable.
  • The lock-in. A double-edged sword: it stops you from selling in a panic (good), but it also traps capital if the plan underperforms or you need liquidity (bad). You could replicate the strategy with a normal flexi-cap + hybrid and retain full liquidity.
  • Who it's for. Disciplined retirement savers who want the commitment device and won't miss the liquidity; Pure Equity plan for those 10+ years from retirement.
05Risks
  • Illiquidity. 5-yr / till-retirement lock-in — no early exit; a real cost if life or the fund disappoints.
  • No tax benefit. Lock-in without an 80C deduction, unlike ELSS.
  • Plan-choice risk. Wrong plan for your horizon compounds against you over years.
  • Manager continuity. Solution funds see manager changes — track it.
06Verdict
For
  • Strong Pure Equity record (18.9% 3Y and 5Y).
  • Lock-in enforces retirement discipline.
  • Four plans cover the whole horizon; low cost.
Against
  • Lock-in with no 80C tax benefit; illiquid.
  • Replicable with a liquid flexi-cap + hybrid.
  • Plan-choice and manager-change risk.
BUY
A solid, goal-based retirement SIP — the Pure Equity plan's record is genuinely strong and the lock-in is a useful commitment device if you value forced discipline. But go in clear-eyed: it's a Solution fund with a lock-in and no tax break, so if you'd rather keep liquidity, a plain flexi-cap SIP does much the same job. Match the plan to your years-to-retirement. Re-evaluate on a manager change or a rule change to the lock-in. (Previously "Accumulate · SIP".
