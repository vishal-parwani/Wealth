Verdict

The form that stood out in July has cooled to mid-table. There are still only 2.8 years of history and no bear market survived yet. Helios is Samir Arora's AMC, with Alok Bahl and Pratik Singh running this scheme. In July its 1Y return (9.65%) was the best in the comparison set. Since then the NAV has fallen 2.2% (₹16.25 → ₹15.89) while MO Large & Midcap rose 1.8%. Its 1Y is now 3.0%, third of seven, behind MO Large & Midcap (5.6%) and ICICI Retirement Pure Equity (5.2%). Since launch it compounds at 16.8% a year, still behind those two over the identical window (21.6% and 20.1%). Still a reasonable starter-SIP candidate. The last two months are a reminder of why conviction sizing should wait for a full cycle. Hold.

Why it's interesting

  • Still ahead of most of the peer set: 3.0% over 1Y beats four of the six comparators in a weak market, though it's no longer the leader it was in July.
  • Pedigree: Samir Arora's three-decade public-equity record (Alliance, Helios Singapore) informs the house process; "elimination investing" gives the fund a repeatable, articulable discipline rather than star-manager dependence.
  • Healthy vitals for a young fund: ₹7,094 cr gathered in 2.6 years, 0.48% direct TER (competitive for an active flexi-cap), no capacity concerns at this size.
  • Middle-of-road risk so far: −18.2% max drawdown sits between MO's −26.1% and ICICI's −16.9% over the identical window.

Risks & watch items

  • No full cycle. Launched into a bull market; the deepest test so far is a −18% correction. Style discipline in a 2008/2020-grade drawdown is unproven — the main reason for the Watch rating.
  • Lifetime lag vs the leaders: 19.1%/yr since launch trails both MO Large & Midcap and ICICI Retirement Pure Equity (~22.5%/yr) over the same window; the recent outperformance is months old, not years.
  • Named managers are not the marquee name: Arora is founder/CIO, but day-to-day management is Bahl/Singh — key-person risk cuts both ways.
  • New AMC operational track: Helios MF itself dates from late 2023; processes, service and stress behaviour are young.
