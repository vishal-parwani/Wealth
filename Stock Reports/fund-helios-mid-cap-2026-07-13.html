Verdict

Spectacular start, sixteen months of evidence. Since its March 2025 launch the fund has compounded at 28.9% annualised and its 1-year return (16.23%) laps the established mid-cap franchises — HDFC Mid Cap did 7.1% and Motilal Oswal Midcap went negative over the same year. The same Bahl/Singh team runs the older Helios Flexi Cap, which shows the house process travels. But the fund launched near a market bottom, has never seen a bear market, and is already running hotter than peers on drawdown — this is a promising starter-SIP candidate, not yet a conviction holding.

Why it's interesting

  • Clear early alpha: +7.5 pts over HDFC Mid Cap and +20 pts over MO Midcap since launch — in a period that punished the momentum style hard, which suggests the outperformance isn't just a style tailwind.
  • Proven team, second act: Pratik Singh and Alok Bahl also run Helios Flexi Cap (top-of-peer-set 1Y form) — the mid-cap fund extends an already-working process rather than betting on a new one.
  • Small and nimble: ~₹1,500–1,800 cr in a category where the incumbents run ₹70,000+ cr books; small size is a genuine edge in mid-caps, where position-building moves prices.
  • Reasonable cost: ~0.60% direct TER — higher than giant incumbents but fair for a small active book.

Risks & watch items

  • 16 months is not a track record. The fund launched near the March 2025 market bottom — every since-launch number enjoys the most favourable possible starting point. First real test still ahead.
  • Runs hot: max drawdown of −16.7% already exceeds HDFC Mid Cap's −12.6% over the identical window — expect volatility nearer the MO end of the spectrum in a downturn.
  • Category risk: mid-cap valuations remain elevated versus history; category-wide drawdowns of 25–30% are normal once a cycle turns, regardless of manager skill.
  • AUM figures vary by source (₹1,490–1,793 cr) and the AMC's operational history is short.
