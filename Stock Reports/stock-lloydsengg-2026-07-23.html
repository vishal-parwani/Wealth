02Business & Qualitative

Model. Designs and builds heavy/critical engineering equipment and provides EPC across infrastructure, ports, oil & gas, steel-plant and industrial projects, organised into Infrastructure, Engineering and Electrical segments. Part of the broader Lloyds group (Lloyds Metals & Energy is a promoter-group company holding ~4.6%).

Strategy — EPC roll-up. FY26 was a transformation year: merger of Lloyds Infrastructure, Metalfab Hightech and Techno Industries into the listed entity (CCI-approved), plus a string of technology tie-ups — TMW (EPS Gen-4 eco-steel tech), TB Global / expanded Fincantieri alliance (marine loading arms, CPP, shafting → shipbuilding), FlyFocus (drones) and CEMI (process optimisation). A further SISCOL / Steel Infra acquisition (~₹1,073–1,220 Cr, 88%) is pending (EGM 15 Jul 2026), targeting ~₹10,000 Cr revenue by FY30.

Moat. Limited/emerging — EPC and heavy fabrication are competitive and execution-driven; the differentiation bet is on licensed technologies (Fincantieri shipbuilding, TMW steel) and capacity scale (~2x at Murbad).

10Verdict

SPECULATIVE · ROLL-UP EXECUTION RISK
An ambitious EPC consolidation story with a real order book, but the M&A pace, dilution and accounting complexity make it a story you must underwrite on execution, not optics. Re-evaluate on: SISCOL deal close & terms, post-merger consolidated margins, organic vs inorganic growth split, and promoter-holding trajectory.
