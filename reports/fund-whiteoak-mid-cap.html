Thesis in one paragraph

A young (Sep-2022) but impressive mid-cap fund from Prashant Khemka's WhiteOak Capital (Khemka formerly ran Goldman Sachs' India and Global EM equity businesses). The house runs a deliberately balanced, stock-picker's portfolio: ~100+ holdings, small active bets, pro-cyclical and counter-cyclical exposures paired off so that returns come from stock selection rather than macro or style bets. Since launch the fund has compounded at roughly 24% CAGR (₹10 → ₹23.11 in ~3.8 years) and has beaten its benchmark over both 1-year and 3-year windows — the mirror image of the concentrated-bet approach of peers like Motilal Oswal Midcap.

Portfolio & style

  • Highly diversified: top holdings — Max Financial Services, Federal Bank, Phoenix Mills, Fortis Healthcare, Bharti Hexacom — each carry small weights; single-stock blow-up risk is low by construction.
  • Sector spread: financials, healthcare, industrials, consumer cyclicals, real estate, telecom — no dominant sector bet.
  • Mandate: ≥65% in mid-cap stocks at all times; bottom-up selection, "buy great businesses at attractive valuations" with balanced factor exposure.

People & house

  • Ramesh Mantri (CIO) is lead manager, with Trupti Agrawal, Ashish Agrawal, Dheeresh Pathak and Piyush Baranwal (debt). Team-run, process-driven — less key-man risk than a star-manager fund.
  • WhiteOak's institutional pedigree (Khemka's GS background, large offshore India book) predates the MF licence; the AMC's processes are older than the 2022 fund.

Strengths / Risks

  • + Benchmark-beating in both up and down phases so far; alpha from stock-picking, not one hot sector.
  • + Cheap direct plan (~0.5%), tiny minimums, short 1-month exit load.
  • + Diversification suits a core mid-cap allocation.
  • − Under 4 years of live history; hasn't been tested through a full bear cycle at scale.
  • − AUM is scaling fast; mid-cap alpha typically compresses with size.
  • − Mid-cap valuations remain rich in 2026 — category risk, not fund-specific.

Verdict

Accumulate · SIP. The best risk-adjusted way to own this category right now: diversified construction, live outperformance, low cost, credible team. Prefer SIP/staggered entry over lumpsum given stretched mid-cap valuations. For fresh mid-cap money it currently screens better than Motilal Oswal Midcap (see companion report), which is mid-transition after its lead manager's exit. Revisit after the fund crosses ~₹10,000 Cr AUM to check whether alpha persists.
